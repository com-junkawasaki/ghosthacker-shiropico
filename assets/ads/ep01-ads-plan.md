## Setup (per campaign, Google Ads)
1. New campaign → **Goal: Awareness/Consideration** → **Campaign type: Video** → **Video views**.
2. Budget: **Campaign total ¥3,000** (run ~7 days, ~¥430/day) · Bidding **Max CPV ≈ ¥6**.
3. Networks: YouTube videos (in-stream skippable) + YouTube search/in-feed.
4. **Languages**: set the row's lang. **Locations**: the row's geo list.
5. Audience: anime / cybersecurity / parenting-tech affinity. **Do NOT target under-18 directly** (kids-content ad policy).
6. Ad: paste the video URL, headline, long headline, description, CTA from the row.
7. Review → Publish (this is the billing/spend step — owner only).

## Notes
- zh targets TW/HK/SG/MY (mainland CN has no YouTube).
