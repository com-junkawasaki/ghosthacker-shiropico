# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 6: "Don't Lose the Key"

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Field Guide Corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: IAM/PAM (Least Privilege & Key Management) + MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)
**Today's Blunder**: Password reuse gets everything cracked at once
**Setting**: Singapore — "Golden Key Tower," the nerve center of a smart city

---

## 🎵 OP (10 sec · same every episode)
Mini SHIRO & PICO spin and dance across a teal cityscape. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: The Morning the Keys Vanish

**[AERIAL SHOT — Singapore, just before dawn]**
A luminous grid floats above the sea. At the heart of the skyline, a golden key-shaped monument blazes with light — **Golden Key Tower** — the beating heart that manages every smart system in the city.

**[Inside the Tower — Control Room]**
Monitors cover every wall. **Om** (a soft-spoken, slightly round middle-aged man, glasses, coffee cup in hand) is on the night shift, stifling a yawn.

**OM:** "Hahhh… another peaceful night…"

*PING!* Every screen flashes red.

**MONITOR:** "⚠️ ADMIN LOGIN — AUTHENTICATION SUCCESSFUL"

**OM:** "Wait — someone just logged in? …That wasn't me."

In the next instant — **every door in the Tower begins to click open at once.** Vaults. Server rooms. Power panels. Every single "lock," silently, one by one, comes undone.

**[Street level]** A traffic-control box panel flips open on its own.
**[Harbor]** The port entry gate glides smoothly upward.
**[Hospital]** The pharmaceutical storage room's electronic lock pops with a soft click.

**OM:** *(voice trembling)* "The keys… every single one… they're being stolen…!"

**[Above the city]** Against the night sky, dozens of **golden key-shaped silhouettes** rise — then vanish, drawn toward a single point.

---

## Scene 2 | The Blunder Pattern ①–③ (Show Off → Mess Up → Panic)

**[In front of the Tower — just after dawn]**
SHIRO and PICO arrive on the scene in Mini Mode. Om is waiting at the entrance, pale as a sheet.

**OM:** "Please, you have to help! Every lock in the city was opened through a single account —"
**PICO:** "Keys, huh? Easy. I've always been great with keys, just so you know? **Leave it to me!**" ← **Show Off**

SHIRO glances sideways at PICO. Says nothing.

**PICO:** "First, we seal the entry point! Om, if you give me the admin password, I'll reset everything right away!"
**OM:** "Oh, of course. It's `golden1234`. Ah — actually, the elevator uses the same one. And the server room too, come to think of it —"
**PICO:** *(stopping dead)* "…All the same?"
**OM:** "It's easier to remember…"

PICO freezes for a beat — then bounces right back.

**PICO:** "W-well! We'll just change them all at once! That fixes everything! Technique — **Key Overwrite!**" ← **Blunder — Part One**

PICO hammers the tablet furiously. Every system's password is bulk-changed to "`pico_strongest_2099`" —

**[Instant]** Every panel in the Tower lights up green.

**PICO:** "Nailed it! I unified everything under my password — we're totally safe now —"

*BOOM.*

**MONITOR:** "⚠️ UNAUTHORIZED LOGIN — AUTHENTICATION SUCCESSFUL ×47"

**PICO:** "…What?"

The numbers on the monitor explode upward. 47… 98… 203…

**OM:** "It's going up! The number is going UP?!"
**PICO:** "Why?! I changed it! — Oh no… wait… that password I just used… I already had it somewhere else…" ← **Blunder — The Core**

PICO's face drains of color.

**PICO:** "Wait — wait wait wait — if I reused it, then cracking one means cracking ALL of them?! Every door is flying open!!" ← **Panic**

PICO bounces around in Mini Mode in full meltdown. A red counter appears top-right: **"45 / 44 / 43…"** (locks remaining to be secured).

**PICO:** "No no no, why does anyone even HAVE my password — oh, I think I used this one… somewhere… a long time ago…"

PICO's eyes go distant for just a moment. **A flash of something from the past — but there's no time to chase it.**

---

## Scene 3 | SHIRO's Quiet Save + Transformation Sequence

**SHIRO** steps up beside PICO without a sound. The counter reads **"38."**

**SHIRO:** "— Pico."
**PICO:** "I know! I know, but it won't stop —"
**SHIRO:** *(quiet, but clear)* "Reusing passwords is the same as using **one key for every door.** A thief steals one key — and it's all over."
**PICO:** "…"
**SHIRO:** "What we need to do right now isn't change everything at once. It's sorting out **who's allowed to hold which key.** That's how **IAM** works."
**PICO:** "I… A… M…" *(blank stare)*
**SHIRO:** "Explanation later. — Time to go for real."

PICO clenches both fists.

**PICO:** "…Got it. I'm in."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(same footage every episode)*
High-five — **"Shiro-Pico, ON!"** — cocoon of teal light — height upgrade (5-head proportion) — signature pose (SHIRO: one finger raised / PICO: forward lean, lime-green eyes sharpening to a fierce glow).

## Scene 4 | Two-Layer Action: Cyberspace + The Key Thief

### ◆ The Villain Appears

**[Cyberspace]**
Deep within the Tower's server banks — through a fog of data — the **Key Thief** materializes.

> **🔓 THE KEY THIEF (KEY STEALER)**
> A short, stocky little mech-gremlin covered head to toe in **keys of every shape and size**, jangling and glittering.
> Every key gleams **bright gold** — it looks almost cute, but those eyes are sharp and sly.
> The top of its head has a large keyhole; every new key it slots in makes its body grow bigger.
> Sound effect: *"Jingle-jingle ♪ Jingle-jingle ♪"*

**KEY THIEF:** "Jingle-jingle ♪ — You had sooo many copies of the same key~ I'll just take them allll~" *(cheerful, sing-song voice)*

Every key on its body glitters at once. Doors across every floor of the Tower swing open in a cascade.

**PICO:** "That's the one behind all this! — It's huge! Are all those keys from Om's systems?!"
**SHIRO:** "Yes. **Because the same password was reused everywhere, stealing one copy was enough to get them all.** The Key Thief isn't the real problem — the real problem was careless key management."

### ◆ PICO Charges In — and Fails

**PICO:** "Then I'll just take every key back! **Pico Key Snatch!**"

PICO rockets in at full speed, grabbing at the keys dangling off the Key Thief's body.

But — the Key Thief just grins.

**KEY THIEF:** "Jingle ♪ — They're all the same key, so it doesn't matter which ones you grab~"

PICO yanks one free — and a different key on the body opens the same door. **There are infinite copies.**

**PICO:** "What — it's making copies?!"
**SHIRO:** "That's what password reuse does. **When the same key exists in dozens of places** — pull one away, and the rest still work."

### ◆ SHIRO's Solution: IAM + PAM

**SHIRO** closes their eyes. A **map of light** unfolds across cyberspace — an access-permission chart for every system in the Tower.

**SHIRO:** "**First, we decide who is allowed to open which door. Anyone who doesn't need a key doesn't get one.** — That's **IAM.** Identity and Access Management."

SHIRO sweeps a finger through the air — and keys begin **vanishing** from the Key Thief's body one by one. The pharmaceutical storage key. The harbor gate key. The power panel key. Each one reassigned to exactly the person who actually needs it.

The Key Thief's body shrinks, visibly, before their eyes.

**KEY THIEF:** "Huh? Jingle… jingle… my keys… they're disappearing…"

**PICO:** "Yes! Now I just have to —"

PICO lunges — but one key remains, lodged in the keyhole on the Key Thief's head: a single, **exceptionally thick golden key.**

**KEY THIEF:** "This one… this one I keep… it's the admin key… jingle ♪"

The door to the Tower's core — the central server — begins to swing slowly open. Counter: **"10 / 09 / 08…"**

**SHIRO:** "Pico, stop it! That key — it's a **privileged access** key. The last one."
**PICO:** "Privileged…?"
**SHIRO:** "**The most powerful key needs the most protection.** That's **PAM** — Privileged Access Management. But right now — **we add one more layer of verification!**"

### ◆ The MFA Strike

**SHIRO** reaches into their coat and produces a **small teal shield-shaped badge.** Six digits flash bright across its face.

**SHIRO:** "**MFA — Multi-Factor Authentication. The key alone isn't enough to open the door. You need one more confirmation.**"

SHIRO holds the badge up to the admin door — and a **wall of teal light** materializes in front of it.

The Key Thief jabs the thick golden key toward the lock — and the wall of light *repels it.*

**KEY THIEF:** "Wha?! I have the key and it still won't open?! Why?! WHY?!" *(spinning in frantic confusion)*

**PICO:** "Now — !"

**[Combo Charge-Up]**
PICO and SHIRO stand back-to-back. PICO charges a lime-green aura — SHIRO quietly draws teal energy inward, focusing it to a point —

**PICO:** "We're taking those keys back — !"
**SHIRO:** "— **Lock Reset.**"

**[Release]** Teal and lime spiral together and slam into the Key Thief. Every key on its body bursts free and scatters —

**[Aftermath]** The Key Thief shrinks, drifts gently downward, and curls into a tiny ball. Every keyhole on its body closes shut. It becomes nothing more than a small, harmless mech-sphere.

**KEY THIEF:** "…Jingle… *(tiny voice)* …nighty-night~" *(falls asleep)*

**[FREEZE FRAME + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]**
The two heroes strike their signature pose against the breaking dawn over the Tower. Brushstroke title card: **"LOCK RESET"**

---

### ◆ Aftermath — With Om

The Tower's doors click shut, one by one, in perfect order.

**PICO:** *(still in full-height mode, turning to Om)* "Om, we're going to make every single password **completely different** from here on. A separate key for every system. — And you need to set up **an extra confirmation step** for every login, no exceptions."
**OM:** "Y-yes… do you think I'll be able to remember them all…?"
**PICO:** "Use a password manager — then you don't have to remember any of them. **The reason people reuse passwords is because they're trying to memorize everything in the first place.**"

SHIRO watches PICO's profile for a quiet moment.

**SHIRO:** *(softly, but unmistakably)* "…Yeah. That's exactly right."

PICO goes just a little pink.

## Scene 5 | C-Part: Ghost Arc Thread + Seeds of PICO's Past

**[Tower Rooftop — Dawn]**
Back in Mini Mode, the two of them stand together. Morning light spreads across the city.

SHIRO traces a finger along the data trail left behind by the Key Thief — and finds a **white thermal imprint.**

**SHIRO:** "…This mark again."
**PICO:** "Ghost?"
**SHIRO:** "Someone showed the Key Thief the way in. **Someone who already knew which passwords were being reused** — and the trace is still here. Data always leaves a physical mark."

PICO stares at the fading imprint without speaking.

**PICO:** "…Hey, Shiro."
**SHIRO:** "Yeah."
**PICO:** "I used to be on the other side. The stealing side."

SHIRO doesn't turn around. Just listens.

**PICO:** "Back when I was a CryptoKiddie. I'd get into other people's accounts, look around for a second, then get out. I told myself it was no big deal. …But after today — seeing how **one stolen key can bring everything crashing down** — I just…"

PICO pulls both knees to their chest.

**PICO:** "…The keys I took back then. Someone's doors might have been wide open because of me."

**SHIRO:** *(quietly)* "…They might have been."

PICO presses their lips together hard.

**SHIRO:** "But today, you told Om the truth. **You told him reusing passwords is dangerous.**"
**PICO:** "…I only knew that because I used to do it myself."
**SHIRO:** "**Knowing it is exactly what lets you teach it.** — And that's enough."

Silence. Morning light wraps around them both.

**PICO:** "…Shiro, how come you never get mad at me?"
**SHIRO:** *(a small pause)* "— **Because I'd rather protect what comes next than be angry about what already happened.**"

PICO smiles — just a little.

---

Wide view — the Tower's golden key monument. For just one instant, **Ghost's silhouette** overlaps it. Static. Then it scatters.

*Ghost's voice (a whisper — barely audible):* "— **There is one more key.**"

## 🔎 Field Guide Corner (30 sec · channel style)

Back in Mini Mode, the two stand in front of a whiteboard. Written on it: **"🔑 IAM / PAM / MFA"**

**PICO:** "So what even IS 'I-A-M'?"
**SHIRO:** "**IAM stands for Identity and Access Management — it's the system that decides who's allowed to open which door. You give each person only the key they actually need. That's called Least Privilege.** Tools like Okta and Microsoft Entra ID do this."
**PICO:** "And 'P-A-M'?"
**SHIRO:** "**PAM stands for Privileged Access Management — it means keeping the most powerful keys, like admin accounts, under extra-strict control. CyberArk and Delinea are built for that.**"
**PICO:** "And the shield badge?"
**SHIRO:** "**MFA — Multi-Factor Authentication. Instead of just a key, you need one more proof that it's really you. Even if your password gets stolen, MFA keeps the door shut.**"
**PICO:** "…I'm changing all my reused passwords today." *(looks straight at the camera, flashes a peace sign, expression slightly sheepish)*
**SHIRO:** "**If you want to learn more, head over to the Shiro-Pico Cyber Hub!**" *(PICO hops into frame and waves at the camera)*

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

**Action Design**: Full deployment of the technique-name / charge-up / release / aftermath / freeze-frame structure (*Demon Slayer* / *Naruto* style). Techniques: Key Overwrite → Pico Key Snatch → Lock Reset.
**Blunder Pattern**: Show Off (brash claim that reuse is no problem) → Mess Up (bulk change makes things worse; the root cause of reuse is exposed) → Panic (logins multiply) → SHIRO's Quiet Save (introduces IAM as a concept).
**Series Arc**: PICO's confession — "I used to be on the stealing side" — is the emotional peak of Episode 6. His old habit (CryptoKiddie-era password theft) directly mirrors today's lesson. Ghost's "there is one more key" bridges to the next episode.
**Authentic Cyber Content**: IAM (Least Privilege / Okta / Entra ID), PAM (Privileged Access / CyberArk), MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) — all abstracted as fantasy techniques, with the key metaphor accessible to children and real product names providing a second layer for adult viewers.
**7+ Safe**: Defeat ≠ destroy (the Key Thief says "nighty-night" and goes to sleep). Zero blood, zero terror, zero religious or occult imagery. The Key Thief is an endearing "jingle-jingle" creature.
**Growth Ladder**: Episode 1 (EDR/SIEM) → Episode 6 (IAM/PAM/MFA); PICO's past is made concrete for the first time. "Knowing it is exactly what lets you teach it" — SHIRO's affirmation functions as recognition of PICO's growth.
