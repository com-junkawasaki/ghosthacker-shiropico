# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 9: "Old Habits"

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Field Guide) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber Topic**: Social Engineering (attacks that exploit the human heart) + Victim Support (user education & incident response)
**Today's Blunder**: Making an exception out of sympathy — and opening a hole
**Story Arc**: Emotional peak — reunion with a victim, and atonement

---

## 🎵 OP (10 sec — same every episode)
Chibi SHIRO & PICO spin and dance in a teal cityscape. The title logo *SHIRO & PICO* assembles itself from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Mumbai — Old Quarter, Morning

**[AERIAL SHOT]** A lively street lined with colourful signboards. Laundry flutters in the breeze down a narrow lane. Steam rises from a chai stall. — A peaceful morning. Or so it should be.

**[MARKET LANE]** Outside a vegetable stall. The shopkeeper uncle (chibi-style, pleasantly round) clutches his tablet, face gone pale. A red warning window blinks on the screen.

**WARNING WINDOW** *(caption)*: "ALL YOUR DATA IS BEING READ. Send payment NOW — "

**UNCLE:** "Wh-what in the world…?! I didn't do anything!"

**[NEXT DOOR — STATIONERY SHOP]** A girl — AMI, around 10, twin braids, wide eyes, later revealed to be a past victim of PICO's — is minding the shop. She stares at the same kind of screen, frozen.

**AMI** *(barely a whisper)*: "…It's back."

**[SHADOW OF AN ALLEY]** Chibi SHIRO & PICO pop into view. SHIRO scans the district's data particles through half-closed eyes.

**SHIRO:** "…I sense a Social Daemon. The kind that slips in through the gaps in people's hearts."
**PICO** *(looking around)*: "Gaps in their hearts? I thought Daemons only lived inside machines!"
**SHIRO:** "The human heart is a perfectly good system too."

---

## Scene 2 | The Monster Appears — and a Shadow of Things to Come

**[MARKET SQUARE]** The tablets and display screens of shop after shop turn red one by one. Each time, **gear-bodied mechanical sprites — Daemons** — slither out through the edges of the screens. Swinging tiny scales, they creep up to shopkeepers' ears and whisper.

**DAEMON** *(whispering)*: "You're in trouble, aren't you~? Just tell us one little thing and it'll all be fine~. Just one password~."

The shopkeepers wince and sway, almost drawn in.

**PICO:** "Whoa, they're just *asking* nicely! It's not scary-looking, but it's *totally* scary…"
**SHIRO:** "That's social engineering. They don't take by force. **They move your heart — and make you hand it over yourself.**"

**[PICO stops in front of one of the shops.]**
The stationery shop. AMI is alone, being whispered to by a Daemon. She squeezes her hands into fists.

**DAEMON:** "Poor little thing~. Your dad's shop is in real trouble, isn't it~? Tell us what we want and we'll fix everything~."
**AMI** *(trembling)*: "…Dad's shop…"

**PICO** *(struck, as though something just hit him in the chest)*: "…!"
Something flickers across his face — a memory. He shakes it off quickly.
**PICO:** "Alright, leave it to me! I'm a genius hacker — a tiny Daemon like that? One shot!" ← **showing off**

---

## Scene 3 | The Blunder Pattern: ① Bravado → ② Blunder → ③ Panic

**PICO** steps between the Daemon and AMI. He points at the Daemon and fires a burst of light.

**PICO:** "Technique — **Spark Drive!**"

The Daemons shriek and go flying, three of them diving back into a screen.
**PICO:** "See? Easy!" *(smug face)*

…But.

**AMI** *(looking up at him)*: "Um… won't the data in the shops where the Daemons escaped get erased…? My dad's shop data too…?"
**PICO** *(startled)*: "Oh… wait, it's fine! I can — "

AMI's eyes begin to glisten. PICO wavers. ← **The core blunder approaches.**

**PICO** *(quietly, to SHIRO)*: "Hey SHIRO, couldn't we let this girl's shop go first — let me peek at the data inside and fix it? Just this once, as an exception — "
**SHIRO** *(calm)*: "…PICO."
**PICO:** "She's in trouble! It's just one shop!"

Before SHIRO can stop him, PICO connects to AMI's shop tablet. "I'm only looking, it'll be fine." ← **The blunder.**

**BANG —** The tablet screen **explodes** red. Daemons pour out all at once. Every monitor on the market lane falls like dominoes, turning red. The Daemons **more than double** in number.

**PICO:** "Wha — why?! I only meant to do one shop — !" ← **Panic**
**SHIRO:** "…When you make an exception, that exception becomes a hole. The Daemons were waiting for a gap."
**PICO:** "Nooo! They're multiplying! More and more and MORE — !"

Chibi-mode flailing. He runs in circles. The Daemons spread across the market lane in a wave of whispers. Shopkeeper after shopkeeper sways, on the verge of typing in their personal information. A counter appears top-right: **"05 / 04 / 03…"** (blinking red).

---

## Scene 4 | SHIRO's Quiet Save — and the Transformation

**SHIRO** steps in front of PICO without a sound. Not a sigh — just a quiet narrowing of the eyes.

**SHIRO:** "PICO. Sympathy matters. But **if you open a hole out of sympathy, you can't protect the very thing you're trying to protect.**"
**PICO:** "…I know, but — "
**SHIRO:** "If you know, then let's do what needs doing. — I'm going all in."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(same every episode)*
High-five → "**SHIRO-PICO, ON!**" → Teal cocoon of light → Height upgrade (5-head proportion) → Signature poses (SHIRO: one finger raised / PICO: forward lean — though his expression is a little tighter than usual).

---

## Scene 5 | Action: Two-Layer Battle (Cyberspace × Physical Space)

### ── Layer One: Physical Space (The Market Lane)

The two in full form. Daemons flood every alley of the market.

**PICO:** "There are too many… I can't chase them all!"
**SHIRO:** "We don't need to. **Daemons can't move without someone to whisper to. Protect the people first.**"

SHIRO spreads both arms wide, deploying a **barrier of light — "Wall of Warning"** — in front of the shopkeepers. A small icon lights up above each person's head (🔔 "Watch out!"). Every time a Daemon tries to whisper, the barrier pings it away.

**SHIRO:** "If people are **aware**, Daemons can't get in. — **Knowledge is the strongest shield.**"

**PICO** *(sprinting)*: "Then I'll — !"

PICO draws the Daemons toward himself. He tears through the alleys at full speed, pulling their attention onto him.

**PICO:** "Over here, over here~! I'm way more interesting~!"
The Daemons murmur "ooh, that one looks fun~" and begin chasing PICO, drifting away from the shopkeepers.

### ── Layer Two: Cyberspace (Severing the Root)

**SHIRO** closes his eyes and sinks his awareness into cyberspace. The logs of the entire district rise around him as threads of light — **the SIEM's-eye view.**

**SHIRO:** "…There's a source sending the whispers. Something is broadcasting the same script to every single Daemon — "

The threads of light converge on a single point. At the far end of the market lane: an old electrical junction box. Clinging to it is the **Daemon's ringleader — the Master Daemon.** Its gears are larger. Its scales sway with an unsettling weight.

**SHIRO** *(quiet, but razor-sharp)*: "Found you. **Cut the root, and everything stops.**"

---

## Scene 6 | Emotional Peak — The Phantom and the Reunion

As SHIRO moves toward the junction box — **the Master Daemon swings its scales and projects something.**

A hazy image takes shape in cyberspace.

A small boy. Grey, wild hair. — **A young PICO.**

The phantom PICO is standing in front of the very same stationery shop AMI is in now. He holds a tablet. He taps away at it, quietly pulling the shop's data out. No remorse on his face. Grinning, "Easy~."

**[PICO, pulled into cyberspace, sees the phantom and freezes.]**

**PICO:** "…Oh."

The phantom boy turns around. PICO's own face.

**PHANTOM PICO** *(cheerfully)*: "Something you need? I'm just grabbing some data worth a bit of money. Nobody's gonna get hurt."
**PICO:** "…Nobody's gonna get hurt. That's what I used to think."

**[Physical space — in front of the same stationery shop.]**
AMI notices PICO staring blankly into cyberspace and walks over.

**AMI:** "…Excuse me."
**PICO** *(snapping back to reality)*: "Ah — sorry, it's noth — "
**AMI:** "Have you been here before? …Three years ago. When the data in our shop disappeared."

**Stillness.**

**PICO:** "…What?"
**AMI** *(quiet, not angry — just looking straight at him)*: "My dad was really upset. All the customer names and addresses — everything was gone. …Someone had taken it, he said."

The colour drains from PICO's face.

**PICO:** "…That was me." *(voice barely there)* "…Three years ago, in this town, I — "

AMI looks down slightly. She doesn't shout. She's just quiet.

**AMI:** "…I see."

**A long silence.**

**PICO** *(fists clenched, voice shaking)*: "…I'm sorry. I'm so sorry. Back then I had no idea what I was doing. I never thought about who might get hurt — "

His voice breaks.

**AMI** *(after a pause)*: "…You're going to stop the Daemons, right?"

**PICO:** "…Yeah." *(He lifts his head. Eyes red. But clear.)* "**This time, I'm using it to protect people.**"

---

## Scene 7 | Climax: Combined Technique — Purging the Daemon

**SHIRO** *(standing at the junction box, waiting calmly)*: "…Ready?"
**PICO** *(running up. His expression has changed — the breezy act is gone, replaced by something solid)*: "Sorry for the wait."
**SHIRO** *(small, but unmistakable smile)*: "Good."

The **Master Daemon** swings its scales in a wide arc — one last stand. Every monitor on the market lane flashes red simultaneously. Counter: **"03 / 02…"**

**SHIRO:** "PICO. **Before we cut the root, we pull every whisper-script into the light.** We make the hidden commands visible — **Forensic Ray!**"

SHIRO points one finger at the junction box. **A white line of light streaks forward.** The "whisper scripts" the Daemon had been sending to every shop float up into the air — "Tell me your password." "Send the money." "Click here." — every word exposed, laid bare in the light.

**PICO:** "I can see all of it — !"

PICO takes a running start. Full speed.

**PICO:** "Don't underestimate my old habits. …**The ones I'm using for my friends now!**"

**Technique: "Root Cut" — !**

PICO crosses both hands and drives them into the axle of the Master Daemon's gears — the very root — with pinpoint focus. **The gears spin in reverse.** The scales go *clunk* — and settle perfectly level.

**SHIRO** *(quietly, but with absolute certainty)*: "— **It's all visible now.**"

One finger touches the Daemon's core. **A wave of teal light ripples outward from the junction box, spreading across the entire market lane.**

The Daemons dissolve one by one with a soft "fwaaah~," melting into particles of light. The red drains from the monitors, and one by one they return to a calm, healthy blue.

**[FREEZE FRAME + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]**
The two strike their finishing pose against a dawn sky. Brushstroke text: *"Root Cut."*

**Shopkeepers stir: "Huh? It's fixed!" "The data's back!" AMI watches PICO, relief on her face.**

---

## Scene 8 | C-Part: Atonement, Ghost's Thread, Seeds of Growth

**[Market lane — in front of the stationery shop]**

The clean-up is done. A quiet moment. PICO stands before AMI. SHIRO is a little way off, leaning against a wall, looking at the sky.

**PICO:** "…Hey." *(He bows his head.)* "I wanted to say it properly. I'm really sorry."
**AMI** *(after thinking for a moment)*: "…Tell my dad."

**PICO:** "…Okay."

**[Inside the stationery shop.]** AMI's father — broad-shouldered, but with kind eyes — is sorting through stock. PICO steps inside.

**PICO:** "Um… three years ago, the person who took the data from this shop… was me. I'm sorry."

The father goes still. A long pause.

**FATHER:** "…I see." *(Not angry. But his voice carries weight.)* "Back then, I lost every way I had of reaching my customers. I had to track them all down one by one through the phone book. Took a whole week."
**PICO:** "…"
**FATHER:** "…You're the first one who ever came to apologise."

Another long silence.

**FATHER** *(slowly)*: "…Are you on the side that protects people now?"
**PICO:** "…Yes."

The father places his hand on top of PICO's head.

**FATHER:** "Then that's enough. — AMI, get our guest some tea."
**AMI** *(from the doorway)*: "Coming~." *(A small smile.)*

Tears well up in **PICO**'s eyes again. But this time, he doesn't let them fall.

---

**[Outskirts of the market lane — dusk]**

SHIRO and PICO walk side by side.

**PICO:** "…SHIRO."
**SHIRO:** "Yeah."
**PICO:** "I didn't make that exception just because I felt sorry for her. …When I saw her, something from a long time ago got stirred up inside me. Like everything got mixed around."
**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "I know."
**PICO:** "Why didn't you stop me?"
**SHIRO:** "…Because it has to mean something. And it only means something if you figure it out yourself."

PICO is quiet for a moment.

**PICO:** "…That's frustrating." *(But the corner of his mouth lifts, just slightly.)* "And somehow… I feel lighter."
**SHIRO:** "That's what atonement feels like."

**[Last beat — SHIRO stops and looks into the shadow of an alley.]**

There: **a white heat-trace.** A residue that should not exist after a Daemon is gone. Unexplained.

**SHIRO** *(eyes narrowing)*: "…The phantom was designed to shake PICO. Someone used the Daemon as a tool. **Ghost.** — They were watching everything today."
**PICO:** "…The phantom too? That was them?"
**SHIRO:** "Most likely. **Ghost knows PICO's past.** They tried to use it as a weapon."

PICO's expression sharpens.

**PICO:** "…So they know what I'm capable of?"
**SHIRO:** "Look at it the other way — Ghost considers PICO a **threat.** That's why they tried to shake him."

**PICO** *(thinking for a moment, then looking ahead)*: "…Then I just have to get stronger."

SHIRO says nothing. But — **his eyes narrow, just slightly. Like he's pleased.**

**[Final shot]** Deep in the sunset alley, the faceless silhouette of **Ghost** dissolves into noise and vanishes. For just one moment, its outline overlaps with — **the shadow of a young PICO.**

**Caption**: To be continued —

## 🔎 Field Guide Corner (30 sec — channel-style)

Back in chibi form, the two stand at a whiteboard. PICO's eyes are still a little red, but his energy is back.

**PICO:** "So what exactly was 'social engineering' today?"
**SHIRO:** "**Social engineering = stealing information not through technology, but by targeting the human heart.** Things like 'you're in trouble, aren't you?' or 'I'm someone you can trust' — **they move your emotions and make you hand things over yourself.**"
**PICO:** "…So I used to use those same tricks back then." *(sobering thought)*
**SHIRO:** "Two ways to defend yourself. **① Knowledge — if you know the trick exists, you're much harder to fool. ② No exceptions — bend the rules out of sympathy, and that bend becomes a hole.**"
**PICO:** "…That one really hit home."
**SHIRO:** "In the real world, tools like **KnowBe4 and Proofpoint** run 'phishing simulations' — they send fake suspicious messages on purpose, and teach anyone who gets caught. When someone does get hurt, the support system is called **incident response** — Mandiant and IBM X-Force are well-known names in that field."
**PICO:** "So there are tools that protect people's hearts too."
**SHIRO:** "Exactly. — **If you want to learn more, head over to 'Yukkuri Cyber Channel'!**" *(PICO looks straight at the camera and flashes a peace sign — just slightly embarrassed.)*

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

**Emotional arc design**: Three-stage build — the phantom (confronting one's own past self) → reunion with AMI (a victim's quiet words) → the apology to her father (atonement completed) — accumulating emotional weight without over-sentimentalising, no blood, safe for 7+. **Blunder structure**: "Making an exception out of sympathy" = PICO himself embodies the core mechanic of social engineering (exploiting a gap in the heart), creating a double-layered structure. **Action craft**: Technique names (Spark Drive / Forensic Ray / Root Cut), build-up, release, aftermath, freeze-frame finish. **Two-layer battle**: Physical (market lane) = protective shield + decoy role / Cyberspace (SIEM-style overview) = severing the root. **Ghost's story thread**: The phantom sequence establishes that Ghost knows PICO's past = a hook for the mystery ahead. **Seeds of growth**: The line "this time, I'm using it to protect people" pays off a thread from Episode 1 and sets the stage for the final act.
