# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 10: "City of Hooks"

**Runtime**: approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Field Guide) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: Phishing / Smishing defence (sender verification · URL hover · report button)
**Today's Blunder**: Trusting appearances and tapping a link (falling for phishing)
**Series Arc**: Dubai revisited / Ghost's core mystery / seeds of Pico's growth

---

## 🎵 OP (10 sec · same every episode)
Chibi Shiro & Pico spin and dance in a teal cityscape. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Dubai Revisited — Golden Morning

**[AERIAL SHOT]** The same skyline as Episode 1 — but now it's **daytime**. Light ricochets everywhere, turning the city into a glittering gold.
A subtle lower-third reads: **"Dubai, again——"**

**[STREET LEVEL]** Citizens' smartphones chime one after another — *ping♪ ping♪ ping♪*
Close-up on the screens. A message has arrived:

> 📩 **"Congratulations! You have been selected for the city's Special Relief Grant!
> Claim yours now → [city-dubai-special✦✦✦.net]"**

The icon is a near-perfect copy of the official city-hall logo. Font, colours — almost identical to the real thing.
**Citizen A** (middle-aged man): "Oh — a relief grant? Lucky me!" — his finger moves toward the screen.
**Citizen B** (woman): "Oh, I got one too!"
**Citizen C** (child): "Mine too!"

The moment someone taps — a thin **golden thread** slithers out from the device and disappears into some distant darkness. The screen flickers for just a fraction of a second. Nobody notices.

**[IN THE SHADOW OF A BUILDING]** Chibi Shiro & Pico lean against a wall, watching the street. Only Shiro tracks the golden threads with her eyes.

**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "…There are threads. Stretched across everything."
**PICO**: "Huh? What threads?"
**SHIRO**: "All over the city. —— This is a **city of hooks**."

---

## Scene 2 | Blunder Cycle ①–③ (Swagger → Blunder → Panic)

**PICO** *(squinting, seeing nothing)*: "Hooks? I literally can't see anything."
**SHIRO**: "Right. That's what makes them dangerous."
**PICO** *(rolling up his sleeve)*: "Whatever! Sketchy messages? That's totally my territory. I used to send a ton of these back in the day —— and **now** I know exactly how they work! Leave it to me!" ← **Swagger**

Pico whips out a smartphone-shaped device and starts tapping away.
**PICO**: "First, I'm gonna smash every suspicious one! Technique — **SPAM CRUSHER!**"

Fists of light fly everywhere. He **pounds** every suspicious message on every phone in the city, deleting them one after another.

…But. ← **Blunder**

The moment each message is destroyed, a **new one arrives from a different address — even more convincing than before**.
This time the icon looks exactly like *Shiro* — a tiny avatar in a white hood.

> 📩 **"Pico. Urgent. Check this link immediately → [shiro-pico-alert.net]"**

**PICO**: "Oh — it's from Shiro——" ← his hand moves on reflex.
**SHIRO** *(standing right next to him)*: "…I didn't send that."
**PICO**: "What?" *(freezes)*

A **golden thread** begins to slither out from the link Pico almost tapped — Shiro quietly places a hand over his wrist. The thread stops cold.

**PICO**: "Whoa — that was *close* — ! Wait, why did it come with *your* name on it!? What's going on!?" ← **Panic**

And it gets worse. The shockwave from Pico's SPAM CRUSHER has **backfired**.
The deleted messages **shattered and duplicated** — now even more hook-messages are raining down on every device in the city.

**PICO**: "NOOO! They're *multiplying*! Why does hitting them make MORE?!"
*(Chibi mode — he scrambles around in a panic. Citizens everywhere are about to tap their screens. Golden threads weave across the entire city.)*
**PICO**: "Wait — wait wait wait——!"

---

## Scene 3 | Shiro's Calm Correction + Transformation

**SHIRO** *(still, quiet)*: "Pico."
**PICO**: "W-what?!"
**SHIRO**: "—— **Don't hit. Look.**"

Shiro extends one finger and **magnifies** the message on Pico's screen like a lens.

**SHIRO**: "Here. The sender address. After 'city-dubai' — there's a **special character mixed in**. One character off from the real one. —— **It looks real. But it's something else entirely.**"
**PICO**: "…Oh. You're right. I didn't even notice." *(deflated)*
**SHIRO**: "And before you tap any link — **check where it actually goes**. You can figure it out without tapping."
**PICO**: "But why did hitting them make them multiply?"
**SHIRO**: "Because you tried to erase the *symptoms* without cutting off the *source*. **Messages can be copied. But the thread has one root.** —— Cut that."
**PICO**: "…Okay. (Still annoyed.)** *(lips pressed tight)*

Shiro looks at Pico and narrows her eyes — just slightly. *(Is she smiling? Hard to tell.)*

**SHIRO**: "—— Let's do this for real, Pico."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(same every episode)*
High-five —— "**Shiro-Pico, ON!**" —— cocoon of teal light —— height upgrade (5-head proportion) —— hero pose (Shiro: one finger raised / Pico: forward-leaning stance).

---

## Scene 4 | Two-Layer Action: Data Space × Physical World

Full-height mode. The city is now laced with **countless golden threads** — visible only to the two of them.

**PICO**: "There are so many threads. Do they all connect somewhere?"
**SHIRO** *(eyes closed, sensing the whole space)*: "…They all branch from **one trunk**. Like a tree. Sever the root — they all disappear."

**[DATA SPACE LAYER]**
Shiro opens her eyes. Her vision shifts — above the city, the threads merge into a **river of light** flowing toward a communications tower on an old building at the city's centre.

**SHIRO**: "There. —— The **trunk** of the source. I know that place."
**PICO** *(a flash of recognition)*: "—— That building… from Dubai…!" *(Episode 1 flickers through his memory)*

**[PHYSICAL LAYER — PICO'S ROLE]**
But the path to the trunk is blocked. Ghost's decoy messages have been herding citizens, forming a living wall. People walking with their eyes glued to their phones are unconsciously blocking every route.

**PICO**: "We can't get through — too many people. But we can't just shove them aside——"

Pico thinks for a beat. Then — a **grin**.
**PICO**: "—— You know what else I'm good at? Sending *real* messages."

Pico works his device fast. On the city's official public screens, in the real city-hall logo with the correct verified address, a notice appears:

> 🔔 **"WARNING: Fake messages are currently circulating. Please put your phone away. Official information here → [city.dubai.gov] (verified URL)"**

Citizens look up — "Huh?" — and pocket their phones. The path opens.

**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "…Nice."
**PICO** *(glancing away, a little embarrassed)*: "It's nothing. Just old habits."

**[CHARGING THE TRUNK]**
Both of them sprint through the cleared path toward the base of the communications tower.

And there — **Ghost's shadow**.

Unlike any enemy they've faced before, Ghost has no fixed form. One moment it wears **Shiro's face**, the next **Pico's**, the next the face of a **city official** — cycling through shapes, blocking their way. Semi-transparent data particles in cyan (#29D3C2) endlessly form and collapse.

**PICO**: "Ugh — ! Why does it have *my* face?! That's creepy!"
**GHOST** *(in Pico's voice)*: "—— You almost believed it. You were so close to tapping that link."
**PICO**: "Shut *up*!"

**SHIRO** *(quiet, eyes never leaving Ghost)*: "…Changing shape is its power. But——"

Shiro narrows her eyes. No matter what form Ghost takes, her gaze **never wavers**.

**SHIRO**: "—— **Data is physical. Change your shape all you want — the heat stays behind. I can already see your real outline.**"

**[TECHNIQUE — CHARGING]**
Shiro raises one finger. Slowly, slowly — the air around them goes completely still. Each time Ghost shifts form, a **thermal afterimage** layers over the last in Shiro's vision. An outline — begins to emerge.

**[TECHNIQUE — RELEASE]**
**SHIRO**: "—— **HEAT TRACE.**" *(quiet, but certain)*

A thin line of teal light shoots from her fingertip, **tracing** Ghost's true outline with surgical precision — following that one real contour through every disguise.

**GHOST**: "——!"

Unable to shift any further, Ghost is locked — for just a moment — into its **true form**. A mass of semi-transparent data particles. No face. But —— deep within that outline, there is *something*. A single beat of stillness, as though Shiro has just glimpsed something she was not meant to see.

**[TECHNIQUE — AFTERMATH]**
Ghost, outline fixed, **shrinks back** into the base of the communications tower — as if swallowed by it. Every thread retracts toward the root. The golden threads dissolve across the entire city, one by one.

**PICO**: "Did Ghost just — run? Or disappear?"
**SHIRO** *(lowering her hand, quietly)*: "…Ran. Not gone."

**[HERO FREEZE FRAME + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]**
Both strike their hero pose against the golden city. Brushstroke text: *"HEAT TRACE."*

Every fake message vanishes from every citizen's phone. The city reclaims its shine.

---

## Scene 5 | C-Part: Ghost's Core + Seeds of Pico's Growth

Base of the communications tower. Where Ghost disappeared. Shiro kneels and studies the floor.
Again — a **white thermal afterimage**. But this one is different. Within it, for just a fraction of a second, **fragments of text** surface — then vanish.

**SHIRO** *(breath catches)*: "…"

**PICO**: "Shiro? What is it?"
**SHIRO**: "—— **Ghost knows my name.**"
**PICO**: "What?"
**SHIRO**: "That fake message. It used my name to reach you. —— I knew Ghost was aware of me. But——"

Shiro stands, staring at the fragments of the thermal afterimage.

**SHIRO**: "…Today's afterimage had something mixed in. A memory of **somewhere else. Somewhere I used to be.**"

Silence. Shiro stops speaking. *(Weak point: when her past surfaces, words stop coming.)*

**PICO** *(unusually quiet)*: "…Your home, Shiro?"
**SHIRO** *(no answer. But no denial either.)*

A beat.

Pico glances up at the communications tower. Same building. Same antenna that was puppeting the zombie cars in Episode 1.

**PICO**: "—— We're back. Dubai." *(softly)*
**SHIRO**: "Yeah."
**PICO**: "This is where I made a mess of things. Episode 1." *(wry smile)*
**SHIRO**: "You made a mess of things today too."
**PICO**: "I *know*, okay!" *(but he's smiling)*

A short pause — and then Pico's expression goes serious.

**PICO**: "…Hey, Shiro. That message earlier. I almost believed it for a second."
**SHIRO**: "I know."
**PICO**: "The moment I thought it was *from you* — I stopped checking. —— **That's exactly what I used to do to people. Make them trust me, make them tap, take what I wanted.** …I was really the worst."

Shiro says nothing. She just moves to stand beside him, and they look out at the view together. *(Day has become night.)*

**PICO**: "Next time — I'll check. No matter what. **Look at what's inside, not just what it looks like.** …That's what I learned today."

Shiro's mouth curls — just barely — at one corner.

**SHIRO**: "—— Yeah. That's enough." *(quietly)*

The two of them stand side by side, watching the night city of Dubai.

**[FINAL TWO SHOTS]**

① In Shiro's pocket, a fragment of the thermal afterimage lingers as a **tiny cluster of data particles**. Only Shiro notices.

② Far away, in the dark. Ghost's silhouette — this time, **holding its shape**, not dissolving into noise. Just standing there.
No face. But something about it feels like it is **looking back**.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**: *Ghost's true identity — drawing closer.*
**ON-SCREEN TEXT**: *To be continued——*

## 🔎 Field Guide Corner (30 sec · channel format)

Back in chibi form, the two step up to a whiteboard. Pico still looks a little sore about the whole thing.

**PICO**: "I was not cool today… So what even *is* **phishing**?"
**SHIRO**: "**Phishing = a fake message designed to look real, so you'll tap a link and hand over your information.** When it arrives via SMS on your phone, it's specifically called **smishing**."
**PICO**: "But it looks so real you can't even tell!"
**SHIRO**: "That's why there are **three rules**. ① **Check the sender address character by character.** ② **Before you tap any link, check where the URL actually goes** — you can do that without tapping. ③ **If it feels off, hit the report button.** —— For real-world protection tools, well-known ones include **Proofpoint, Abnormal Security, and Microsoft Defender for Office 365**."
**PICO**: "…*Look at what's inside, not just what it looks like.* Huh." *(a small glint in his eye)*
**SHIRO**: "Exactly. —— **If you want to learn more, head over to 'Yukkuri Cyber Channel', okay?**" *(Pico faces the camera and flashes a peace sign — but today, his expression is just a little more serious than usual.)*

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

**Action grammar**: HEAT TRACE (charge = thermal afterimages stack up · release = one precise strike tracing the true outline · aftermath = all threads retract to the root). Hero freeze frame + technique title card. Defeat ≠ rescue (Ghost ran / citizens' fake messages cleared).

**Blunder cycle**: Swagger (I used to send these, so I know them) → Blunder (hitting them causes duplication / almost taps himself) → Panic (they're multiplying!) → Shiro's calm correction (content not appearance / cut the source not the symptoms) → Pico: "Okay. (Still annoyed.)"

**Two-layer structure**: Kids = golden threads, fake messages as visuals, slapstick chaos. Adults = real phishing/smishing mechanics (sender spoofing, URL verification, reporting) + real-world products (Proofpoint, etc.).

**Series arc**: Dubai revisited (same building as Episode 1) gives a mid-series turning-point feel. Ghost knowing Shiro's past is foregrounded. Pico confronts his own history as a former offender and arrives at "look inside, not at appearances" in his own words — seeds of genuine growth. ED variation plants the next Ghost reveal.

**7+ safe**: No blood, no fear imagery, no religious or occult iconography. Ghost = "a mass of data particles that changes shape." Not defeated, not destroyed — it ran.
