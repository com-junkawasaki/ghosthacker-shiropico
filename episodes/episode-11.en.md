# "SHIRO & PICO" Episode 11 — "Data Never Dies"

**Runtime**: approx. 11 min (10 main + 1 Codex Corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: data remanence + first contact with Ghost
**Today's Goof-Up**: trusting "it's deleted" and missing the traces
**Story Arc**: Ghost's first reveal — showing its power / planting the seeds of Pico's growth

---

## 🎵 OP (10 sec — same every episode)
In the teal city, chibi SHIRO & PICO spin and dance. The title logo "SHIRO & PICO" assembles itself out of data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Iceland — a Geothermal Plant Just Before Dawn

**[Aerial view]** A sky close to the midnight sun. A massive geothermal power plant built on a lava plateau. Columns of steam rise quietly. A small caption at the edge of the screen: **"Iceland — Kvöldelsárlei Geothermal Power Plant."**

**[Plant — Control Room]** A wall covered in monitors. Three operators work the night shift, sipping coffee.

**OPERATOR A** (yawning) "Nothing unusual. Another peaceful night."

In that instant — at the edge of a monitor, just for a flicker, a **white heat-afterimage** shimmers. Like noise. Gone in a blink.

**OPERATOR B** "...Did something just show up there?"
**OPERATOR A** "Probably just your imagination. The logs don't show any—"

He checks the log screen. **Pure white. Zero errors. Perfectly clean.**

**OPERATOR A** "See? Nothing there. No problem."

The moment the two look away from the screen — deep inside the plant, **the temperature sensor reading on a pipe slowly rewrites itself.** Just 0.3 degrees. A change almost no one would notice. But—

**[Outside the control room, in the shadows of the corridor]** On the wall, a **faceless human silhouette** surfaces for just a moment. It dissolves into noise and vanishes.

**[Outdoors, the plant's outer wall]** In the faint light of dawn, a small figure in a white hood lightly drops down onto the wall.

**SHIRO** (narrowing her eyes, gazing at the plant, softly) "...There it is."

---

## Scene 2 | The Goof-Up Pattern ①–③ (Show-off → Goof-Up → Panic)

**[Plant — in front of the outer perimeter fence]**

Pico comes pattering up behind, out of breath.

**PICO** "Wait up, SHIRO~! Why'd you suddenly start running?!"
**SHIRO** "We're going in. — Something's off here."
**PICO** "Off how? The logs were all clean, right? There was nothing when we checked just now, so isn't it already cleared?"
**SHIRO** "...You really think so?"
**PICO** "Course I do. If there's nothing, there's nothing."

SHIRO says nothing and hops lightly over the fence. Pico follows.

**[Control Room — Antechamber]**

Pico holds a hand up to the wall terminal and runs a quick scan. The logs pop up.

**PICO** "See? Told ya. Logs are all clean. Zero errors. Perfect! Easy-peasy~, it's already done. I'm a genius, after all." ← **Show-off**

He turns to SHIRO with a smug grin. SHIRO, eyes half-lidded, silently looks toward the **deep pipework** of the plant.

**PICO** "What? It's solved already. Let's go home~"

In that instant — **from deep inside the plant, a single low alarm sounds.** A short beep.

**OPERATOR A** (jumping up) "Wha— the temperature sensor! The pipe pressure is rising?!"
**OPERATOR B** "There's nothing in the logs — why?! It was clean a second ago!"

The monitors start flashing red, one after another. The numbers rewrite themselves one by one. ← **Goof-Up**

**PICO** "Huh. Wha— why?! It was clean! All the logs were wiped!!" ← **Panic**

Pico holds his hand to the terminal again and scans desperately. But **the logs keep getting overwritten and erased.** No matter how he chases them, new numbers rewrite right where the old ones vanished.

**PICO** "Waaah! I can't keep up! Gone! Gone again! Why?! Why is it growing when it's being erased?!"

In chibi mode he scrambles back and forth in front of the terminal. Stomp-stomp sound effects. The red on the monitors spreads.

**OPERATOR A** "Emergency shutdown sequence won't start! The commands won't go through!"

---

## Scene 3 | SHIRO's Calm Follow-Up + Transformation Bank

SHIRO steps forward slowly. Still in chibi mode, she quietly closes her eyes.

**PICO** "SHIRO! Do something! The logs are gone and I can't track them—"
**SHIRO** (without opening her eyes, quietly) "Pico. — 'Gone'... really?"
**PICO** "It's true! The logs—"
**SHIRO** "Not gone. **You're being made to think they're gone.**"

Pico freezes on the spot.

**SHIRO** (slowly opening her eyes) "Information is physical. Erasing always costs energy. — **When you delete something, heat always remains.** Even if the log vanishes, **the mark of its erasure** doesn't."

SHIRO gently rests her right hand on the wall. Her fingertips glow ever so faintly teal.

**SHIRO** "...See. The **white heat** is still here."

For the first time, Pico's eyes catch it — in the corner of the wall, a faint **white haze** drifting. Everywhere a log was erased, everywhere a rewrite ran, in every "trace of deletion," a thin, thin white heat-trace lingers.

**PICO** "...Ah. It's there. Right over there."
**SHIRO** "**Even when you think it's gone, the light remains.** — We follow this. Let's go, Pico."

Pico gives a firm nod.

**🎵 [Transformation Bank]** (shared cut every episode — 3.5 sec)
The two high-five. "**SHIRO-PICO, ON!**" A cocoon of teal light wraps them. They scale up (5–6 heads tall). The cocoon bursts open — **finishing pose.** SHIRO: right hand held quietly in front of her chest, fingertips lit teal. PICO: leaning forward, both arms spread wide, on standby.

---

## Scene 4 | Action, Part One: Tracing the Heat-Marks

**[Plant — Information-Space Layer]**

The two, in full-size mode, run. A staging effect where the physical corridor and the **information-space layer overlap** — over the walls and pipes, the flow of data floats as lines of light.

**SHIRO** "Pico, chase the white heat-marks. You're the faster one."
**PICO** "Leave it to me! — But, this is—"

Running through the information space, Pico scoops up the vanished traces one after another. He grabs the grains of light in his palm and links them together.

**PICO** "Technique — **Heat-Trace Rush!**"

Running at high speed, he connects the white heat-marks from points to lines, from lines to planes. The "shape" of the erased logs rises up, like a reverse playback.

**PICO** "SHIRO! It's connecting! It all leads from here — to the deepest part of the plant, the **Core Server!**"
**SHIRO** "That's the entrance."

**[Plant — Innermost Depths — Core Server Room]**

A heavy door. When SHIRO touches it gently with her fingertips, the lock quietly opens. The door swings wide—

**[Inside the Core Server Room]**

A dim space lined with server racks. But — in its center.

A **haze of white heat** slowly swirls.

Pico instinctively takes a step back.

**PICO** (his voice dropping a little) "...What... is that."

The haze slowly begins to take shape. No face. The outline is blurred. But unmistakably, **a human silhouette** stands there.

**SHIRO** (catching her breath, quietly) "— **Ghost.**"

---

## Scene 5 | Ghost's First Reveal — Showing Its Power

**Ghost** slowly turns toward the two. No face. Particles of white heat, like noise, shimmer while holding a human form.

Silence.

**PICO** "...There it is. The real thing."

Ghost says nothing. It simply raises its right hand, quietly.

In that instant — **every monitor in the Core Server Room turns white at once.** The logs, all of them, vanish.

**SHIRO** (taking a stance) "Pico, keep your distance."
**PICO** "Huh, but—"
**SHIRO** "**Right now.**"

The tone of her voice hardens — a hardness Pico has never heard before. He reflexively backs away.

Ghost moves slowly. Not through physical space — it is **walking directly along the information-space layer.** The "flow of data" between it and SHIRO sputters and breaks apart each time Ghost passes through.

**SHIRO** "— **Visible Thread.**"

SHIRO narrows her eyes and extends her right hand forward. From her fingertips, a thin thread of teal light stretches out. As if to trace Ghost's outline with the thread of light — **she tries to make it visible.**

Ghost touches the thread.

The next instant — the thread is **flung back.** SHIRO takes a step backward.

**PICO** "SHIRO?!"
**SHIRO** (her expression unchanged, but her hand trembling faintly) "...It's strong. With today's tech, I can't see all of it."

Ghost slowly begins to retreat. Not as if fleeing — but **as though it had simply tested them.**

**SHIRO** (moving to give chase, then stopping)

**PICO** "You're not going after it?!"
**SHIRO** "...I can't. Not now."

Ghost's silhouette dissolves into particles of white heat. Just before it vanishes — for a single instant, within Ghost's outline, **it looked as if a single grain of teal-colored light had dwelt there.**

Pico saw it.

**PICO** "...SHIRO. Inside that thing — there was something. A teal light."
**SHIRO** (a long silence, then quietly) "...You saw it?"
**PICO** "Yeah."
**SHIRO** (softly, as if telling herself) "...I see. Information is physical. **Even when it's gone, a trace always remains.**"

---

## Scene 6 | Action, Part Two: Saving the Plant

Ghost is gone. But — the plant's crisis still goes on.

**OPERATOR (over the intercom)** "Control Room! The pipe pressure is near its limit! The emergency shutdown command won't go through!"

**PICO** (switching his mindset) "SHIRO, the plant!"
**SHIRO** "Right. — We put every command Ghost rewrote back the way it was. Pico, that heat-mark data you scooped up earlier — do you still have it?"
**PICO** "I've got it!"
**SHIRO** "That's the 'trace of the correct state.' — The information from before it was rewritten remains as heat. We use that."

Pico spreads out the grains of white heat he gathered in his palm. The "shape" of the logs from before they were rewritten remains there.

**PICO** "Technique — **Heat-Memory Restore!**"

The grains of light flow into the server racks. The rewritten commands return to their original form.

At the same time, SHIRO spreads both arms wide, surveying the plant's entire information space.

**SHIRO** "Technique — **Full-Spectrum Scan** — **scoop up every trace that remains.**"

Teal light spreads across the whole plant. Every place Ghost touched — the numbers it rewrote, the logs it erased, the fake commands it planted — surfaces as a white heat-afterimage.

**SHIRO** "Pico, third rack from the right — there's still some left."
**PICO** "On it!"

Pico dashes at high speed and lays a hand on the rack. He scoops up the heat-mark and restores the original state.

**[Combined Technique]**
**SHIRO and Pico lay their hands together at the same time.**

**BOTH TOGETHER** "— **Double Remanence Reset!**"

Teal and white light blend together, and ripples spread across the entire plant. Every rewritten number returns to normal. The pipe pressure slowly drops.

**OPERATOR A** (in the control room) "Pressure, normalized! Emergency shutdown command — it went through!!"
**OPERATOR B** "The logs are... coming back. What is going on?"

The alarm stops. Silence. Only the sound of steam returns.

**[Finishing freeze-frame + technique-name caption]** Against the plant's steam, the two strike their finishing pose. The brushstroke caption "Double Remanence Reset."

---

## Scene 7 | C-Part: Ghost's Story Arc + Planting the Seeds of Pico's Growth

**[Outside the plant. A dawn sky.]**

Back in chibi mode, the two sit side by side on the outer wall. The horizon brightens. The columns of steam are tinted by the morning light.

A long silence.

**PICO** (hugging his knees) "...SHIRO. Ghost was strong, huh."
**SHIRO** (quietly, still watching the sky) "Yeah."
**PICO** "Your technique got flung right back. Yours, SHIRO."
**SHIRO** "...Yeah."
**PICO** "...Even you can't win?"

SHIRO pauses just a little, then.

**SHIRO** "The me of right now couldn't see all of it. — **Ghost is too good at deleting.** It thins out its own traces almost to nothing and hides them."
**PICO** "But it can't erase them completely, right? You said it yourself just now — information is physical."
**SHIRO** (for the first time, turning toward Pico) "...That's right. **It can never be completely erased.** So someday it'll definitely be seen. — Just like you saw that teal light."

Pico scratches his head, a little embarrassed.

**PICO** "What was that, anyway. That light."
**SHIRO** (turning her gaze back to the distance, softly) "...I don't know yet, either."

Silence again. Then, as if suddenly remembering, Pico.

**PICO** "Hey, SHIRO. You know — way back, I used to be really good at deleting logs. Everything I did, I'd wipe it all clean so I wouldn't get caught. **I thought it was gone.**"

SHIRO listens in silence.

**PICO** "...But today, it's not gone, is it. It all remains. As heat."

Pico looks at his own palm. The hand that, a moment ago, was holding grains of white heat.

**PICO** "...The stuff I did — is that still out there somewhere too, I wonder."

SHIRO doesn't answer. But — just a little, she leans her body toward Pico.

**SHIRO** (quietly) "Let's go, Pico. — On to the next city."

Two tickets flutter. Pico stands up. But today, he doesn't chase right after her.

**PICO** (looking up at the sky, as if to himself) "...If it's not gone, then I've got to face it properly."

Then, he breaks into a run after SHIRO.

**[Final cut]** A distant view of the plant. In the morning light, for an instant — **a haze of white heat forms a human shape, then vanishes.** Ghost's silhouette. And within its outline, for just a single instant — **a grain of teal light dwells once more, then fades.**

## 🔎 Tool Codex Corner (30 sec — channel style)

Back in chibi form, the two stand before a whiteboard. Pico holds out grains of white heat on his palm.

**PICO** "So what's today's 'data remanence'?!"
**SHIRO** "**Data remanence** is the phenomenon where, even after you erase information, physical traces — heat or magnetism — keep on lingering. There's a law of physics called Landauer's Principle: because **erasing always costs energy (heat)**, it can never be fully erased."
**PICO** "So even if a log is deleted, you'll still get caught?"
**SHIRO** "Right. **Forensic tools** — like **Magnet AXIOM** or **Autopsy** — dig out the 'lingering traces' of deleted files and logs. Even when you think it's gone, it'll always be found."
**PICO** "Ghost left traces behind too, huh..."
**SHIRO** "**Information is physical. Even when it looks gone, the light remains. ✨** — Kids who want to know more, head over to the 'Easy Cyber Channel'!"

Pico looks straight at the camera and softly blows the grains of white light off his palm, flashing a peace sign. The particles scatter like stars.

Mid-hide-and-seek, Ghost peeks out, gets embarrassed, and hides again. — But today, inside Ghost's silhouette, **a single grain of teal light remains, and won't fade away.**
