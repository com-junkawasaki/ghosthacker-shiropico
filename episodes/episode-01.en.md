# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 1: "Onem's Rampage, Dubai by Night"

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Field Guide corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: EDR/XDR (isolate endpoints and restore them to normal) + SIEM (collect logs and correlate)
**Today's Mistake**: Treating symptoms (tackling machines one by one)

---

## 🎵 OP (10 sec · same every episode)
Chibi-mode SHIRO & PICO spin and dance in a teal-lit city. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Dubai — The City After Dark

**[AERIAL SHOT]** A futuristic city glowing teal. Peaceful — until every traffic signal in town **flares a sharp, pulsing red**. The driverless autonomous cars rolling through the streets all **go "clunk…" and close their eyes** at once. They begin to drift and weave.

**CAR A** *(drowsy mumbling)* "Mmmhh… next stop… zzz…"

**[STREET LEVEL]** A parent and child stand at a crosswalk. A stroller. A car with its eyes shut drifts toward them — slowly, but surely. Top-right corner of the screen: a **counter "03 / 02 / 01…"** *(flashing red — suspense that needs no words)*.

**[ROOFTOP → FREEFALL]** Chibi-mode SHIRO & PICO peek over the edge of a building and drop off — landing soft as cats on the pavement below.

**PICO:** "Whoa, the whole city's asleep on the job! That's hilarious."

**SHIRO:** *(scanning the street with half-lidded eyes)* "…It's not funny. Everyone's been *forced* to sleep. We have to wake them up."

---

## Scene 2 | The Classic Blunder — Steps ①②③ (Big Talk → Mistake → Panic)

**PICO:** *(rolling up his sleeves)* "Easy mode. I'm a genius hacker, remember? Watch this!" ← **Big Talk**

PICO slams both hands on the ground and unleashes a technique.

**PICO:** "Done and dusted! Technique — ***WILD PING!***"

*FLASH* — a pulse of light radiates outward. Three nearby cars snap awake with a "Hah!" and screech to a halt.

**PICO:** "See? Told you — easy." *(smug grin)*

…But the moment those cars stop, the ones beside them **catch the sleep like a yawn and doze off too**. The number of sleeping cars **keeps growing**. ← **The Mistake**

**PICO:** "Wha — huh?! Why are there *more?!* Every one I wake up, the next one falls asleep?!" ← **Panic**

*(Chibi-mode PICO scrambles around in a frenzy. Loud comic sound effects. The counter hits "02." The parent and child are in serious danger.)*

**PICO:** "Oh no oh no oh no, I'm not gonna make it——!"

---

## Scene 3 | SHIRO's Quiet Save + Transformation

**SHIRO** slips soundlessly between the family and the oncoming car. One arm extends. One finger touches the car's "forehead."

**SHIRO:** "——*Sleep Call.* I'm cutting this one off from the rest."

The car shudders — then: *"Fwaa…? Oh?"* — snaps back to its senses and stops cold. **The family is safe.** *(Wind. Silence. Hair drifting — the moment breathes.)*

**PICO:** "Wh — how did you do that in *one shot?!* Mine just made it worse!"

**SHIRO:** *(without turning around)* "Because you were playing whack-a-mole. Wake them up one by one, the signal that's *controlling* them is still there — they'll just fall back asleep. '*Isolate it, then restore it to normal*' — that's how **EDR** works."

**PICO:** "E… D… R…?" *(blank stare)*

**SHIRO:** "Explanation later. — PICO. We're doing this for real."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(same cut every episode)*
High-five → **"SHIRO-PICO, ON!"** → cocoon of teal light → height increase (5-head proportion) → finishing pose *(SHIRO: one finger raised / PICO: forward lean)*.

---

## Scene 4 | Action: Isolate with EDR, Correlate with SIEM

Full-height mode. Speed lines. But the sleeping cars are now **a swarm of dozens.**

**PICO:** "Ugh, there's way too many. Are we seriously going to touch every single one?!"

**SHIRO:** "No. **Going one by one is whack-a-mole.** We cut off the *source* of the sleep signal."

SHIRO closes his eyes and expands his awareness across the entire city. **Logs from everywhere — streams of light — converge in mid-air to a single point.**

**SHIRO:** "**Gather every scattered log and see them all at once.** …That's **SIEM**. There — the 'command tower' putting everyone to sleep is in that old building." *(The rooftop antenna pulses red — the **C2**.)*

**PICO:** "That red thing is pulling the strings for *all* of them?!"

**SHIRO:** "Exactly. **Don't hit the endpoints. Cut off the head.** — Let's go."

**[COMBINED ATTACK]** PICO sprints down the road, weaving between sleeping cars, drawing their attention.

**PICO:** "Over here, sleepyheads! Come and get me~!" *(The cars line up chasing PICO — the road clears.)*

SHIRO reaches the base of the antenna (C2). He places his fingers against it.

**Both, simultaneously:** "——***DOUBLE WAKE-UP!***"

The red antenna cracks — and turns teal. A ripple spreads across the entire city. **Every car wakes up at once: "Fwaa~! Good morning!"** *(No destruction — they're saved, not defeated.)*

**[FREEZE FRAME + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]** Both strike their finishing pose against the Dubai skyline. Brushstroke text: **"DOUBLE WAKE-UP."**

---

## Scene 5 | C-Part: The Hook + Seeds of the Past

The city is at peace. But — at the spot where the controlling antenna stood, an **inexplicable white heat-trace flickers**, refusing to disappear.

**SHIRO:** *(narrowing his eyes)* "…It should be gone. But the trace is still here. **Data is physical.** This is — a **Ghost.**"

**PICO:** "A Ghost? Like… a haunting?"

**SHIRO:** "A data phantom. Something you thought you deleted — that didn't actually disappear. …And the troublesome part is, this one has started to *move.*"

PICO stares out at the night skyline, and quietly says:

**PICO:** "…Y'know. There was a time when I used to be on the *putting people to sleep* side of things." ← **Origin / backstory foreshadowing**

SHIRO asks nothing. He produces two tickets and lets them flutter.

**SHIRO:** "Let's go, PICO. Next city."

**PICO:** "…Hey, *wait up!*" *(breaks into a jog)*

**[FINAL SHOT]** Far across the skyline, a faceless **Ghost** silhouette dissolves into static and vanishes.
**On-screen text:** *To be continued —*

## 🔎 Field Guide Corner (30 sec · channel style)

Back in chibi mode, the two stand in front of a whiteboard.

**PICO:** "Okay, so what even *was* that 'EDR' thing today?"

**SHIRO:** "**EDR = a tool that watches over your devices and 'isolates and restores' any suspicious one.** Real-world examples include CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, and Microsoft Defender."

**PICO:** "And the thing where you gathered all the light in the sky?"

**SHIRO:** "**SIEM = a tool that collects scattered logs and correlates them.** Think Splunk, or Microsoft Sentinel. — **EDR *sees* the device and acts on it; SIEM *collects* the logs and gives you the big picture.** They do different jobs."

**PICO:** "…So basically, I grabbed the wrong tool and started whacking one machine at a time, which is why it kept spreading?!"

**SHIRO:** "Exactly. **If you want to learn more, head over to 'Yukkuri Cyber Channel'!**" *(PICO looks straight into the camera and throws up a peace sign.)*
