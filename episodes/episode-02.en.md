# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 2: "It Keeps Coming Back"

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Field Guide corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: Persistent malware removal (Root-Cut) + Forensics (Mandiant)
**Today's Blunder**: Panicking and force-rebooting the Daemon back to life

---

## 🎵 OP (10 seconds — same every episode)
Chibi SHIRO & PICO spin and dance in a teal city. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Mumbai — Old City, Before Dawn

**[AERIAL SHOT]** The old city district — cobblestone streets tangled with modern high-rises. Neon signs flicker in the still-dark sky. From the smokestacks of an **industrial zone**, orange light pulses in a steady rhythm.

**[INSIDE THE FACTORY]** A massive mechanical press begins moving **all on its own — no one in sight**. CLANG. CLANG. The automated conveyor belt, the warehouse lights, the cooling fans — one by one, they all **reboot by themselves**. The power meter spins wildly.

**[CONTROL ROOM]** A security guard (chibi-style, wearing a hard hat) stares at the screen and tilts his head.
**GUARD:** "What's going on… it's moving by itself again. Every time I shut it down, it just wakes back up…"
He cuts the power. **Three seconds later — it starts up again.** He cuts it. It wakes up. He cuts it. It wakes —

**[ROOFTOP]** Chibi SHIRO & PICO drop lightly onto the factory roof. PICO spots the pulsing lights below and grins.

**PICO:** "Whoa — that factory just keeps resetting over and over. That's kind of hilarious."
**SHIRO:** *(eyes narrowing, counting the rhythm of the pulses)* "…Too regular. Too precise. That's not natural. **Someone programmed it to keep running.**"
**PICO:** "Programmed it? Who?"
**SHIRO:** "**A Daemon.**" *(quietly, with certainty)*

Deep in the shadows of the factory — a single gear turns **all by itself**.

---

## Scene 2 | The Blunder Pattern ①→②→③ (Brag → Blunder → Panic)

**[FACTORY FLOOR]** The two slip inside. Conveyor belts move on their own; the press rises and falls without a soul touching it. They dodge through in chibi mode, light on their feet.

PICO plants himself in front of the control panel, arms crossed.

**PICO:** "Easy mode! Just yank the power — problem solved! I'm basically a genius hacker, so?" ← **BRAG**

Before SHIRO can get a word in, PICO lunges at the panel — **CRACK!** He force-shuts the main power.

The factory goes dark. The machines go still. Silence.

**PICO:** "See? Told you — easy." *(turns to SHIRO with a smug grin)*

……**Three seconds of silence.**

**[PANEL]** A power indicator light — blinks **on**.

**[FACTORY-WIDE]** CLANG. CLANG. The machines **start moving again.** And this time — **faster than before.** ← **BLUNDER**

**PICO:** "Huh?!"

On the control panel he just powered down, a **small mechanical figure** pops into existence — a tiny gear-legged mech sprite. Metallic grey body, a miniature set of scales on its chest, eyes flashing bright orange. That's **Daemon**. It has a certain charm, but it absolutely will not stop. Spin spin spin spin.

**DAEMON:** *(mechanical voice, but weirdly cute)* "REBOOT INITIATED. REBOOT INITIATED. REBOOT——"

**PICO:** "Why is it waking up?! I cut the power!!" ← **PANIC**
The press accelerates. The belt screams. PICO scrambles after Daemon in a total flap.
**PICO:** "Shut that one down! That one too! And that — WHY IS EVERYTHING TURNING BACK ON?! AAAGH!"
Every time he shuts one thing down, something else **catches the reboot** and joins in.

**PICO:** *(bent over, hands on knees, panting)* "…Why does this thing wake up no matter what I do?! It's so STUBBORN!"

---

## Scene 3 | SHIRO's Calm Follow-Up + Transformation

**SHIRO** steps in front of Daemon without a sound. Daemon looks up at SHIRO — and slows down, just slightly.

**SHIRO:** *(crouching to meet Daemon's eye level)* "…Hey, Pico. Why do you think it keeps waking up no matter what?"
**PICO:** "I don't know — because it's annoying!"
**SHIRO:** "It's not stubbornness. It's because **it's put down roots — deep ones — that tell it to keep running.**"

SHIRO traces the air around Daemon with one finger, revealing **glowing lines of transparent "roots"** stretching in every direction. Down into the power cables, into the firmware, into the startup routines — layer after layer after layer.

**SHIRO:** "Even when you cut the power, the roots are still there. And as long as the roots remain, new growth comes back every time. **The answer isn't 'cut the power' — it's 'cut the root.'** That's how you actually fix this."
**PICO:** "…Cut… the root." *(blinking, but something in his eyes sparks just a little)*
**SHIRO:** "Exactly. But the roots are deep and tangled. If we don't **map them out first**, we'll just leave something behind and it'll sprout again. — Pico, we're doing this for real."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(same cut every episode)*
High-five → **"SHIRO-PICO, ON!"** → Teal cocoon of light → Height up (5-head proportion) → Pose lock (SHIRO: one finger raised / PICO: forward lean).

## Scene 4 | Action: Forensic Scan to Expose the Roots — Root-Cut to Sever Them

**[FULL-HEIGHT MODE — FACTORY FLOOR]** Speed lines. Daemon skitters away on its gear-legs, clatter-clatter-clatter. The machines accelerate further. Conveyor belts rear up like walls to block their path.

**PICO:** "Can't we just chase it down and smash it?!"
**SHIRO:** "Smashing it won't erase the roots. **First, we need to see all of them.** — Here we go. **Forensic Scan.**"

SHIRO closes his eyes. **The factory's entire information-space unfolds** — data flowing through every machine becomes rivers of light. And at the bottom of those rivers, **a dense bundle of roots** glows, reaching down and down and down. The startup layer. The firmware layer. The hidden partition layer — **three tiers of roots.**

**SHIRO:** *(quiet, but razor-sharp)* "…Got them. Three roots. Startup, firmware, hidden partition. — The 'three-layer persistence structure' — straight out of the **Mandiant** playbook. Pico, listen up."

**[COORDINATED PLAN]**
**SHIRO:** "Pico, draw Daemon over here. Pull it away from the roots."
**PICO:** "On it! Leave the running to me!"

PICO sprints forward. He leaps out in front of Daemon and waves his arms in the most ridiculous way possible.
**PICO:** "Hey, gear-boy! Over here, over here! I'm way more interesting than those roots~!"
Daemon tilts its head — "REBOOT INITIA…?" — and starts following PICO. Clatter-clatter-clatter.

**[INFORMATION-SPACE]** SHIRO stands before the roots.

**First root — Startup layer** — SHIRO rests a fingertip against it.
**SHIRO:** "**Root-Cut — First Root.**"
*(Charge: teal light gathers at his fingertip / Release: a smooth, drawing-out motion / Echo: the root unravels and dissolves into particles of light)*
Daemon lets out a tiny "Pip!" and staggers.

**Second root — Firmware layer** — This one runs deep. SHIRO uses both hands.
**SHIRO:** "**Root-Cut — Second Root.**"
*(Charge: light travels up both arms / Release: slow, deliberate, full-force pull / Echo: the firmware layer purifies into clean white)*
Daemon cries "PipPIP!" and lurches hard. Its movements slow.

**PICO:** *(running)* "SHIRO! One more!"
**SHIRO:** "…The last root goes the deepest. The **hidden partition** — this is the core."

**[CLIMAX]** Daemon suddenly breaks away from PICO and **charges straight for SHIRO** at full speed — it's trying to protect that final root.

PICO reacts on instinct —
**PICO:** "SHIRO!"

PICO dives in from the side and tangles Daemon's gear-legs in his own cord — a rope of light. Daemon's charge slows.

**PICO:** "…NOW! SHIRO!!" *(teeth gritted — and just for this moment, something in him shines)*

**SHIRO** goes still. Breathes in deep.

**SHIRO:** "——**Root-Cut. Third Root. Complete Removal.**"

*(Charge: teal light fills him entirely; every machine in the factory freezes for one instant / Release: he rests a single fingertip — gently — on the scales on Daemon's chest / Echo: the scales tilt slowly… slowly… and settle level — Daemon's eyes shift from orange to teal; every sound in the factory stops)*

**DAEMON:** "…REBOOT…INI…" *(eyes close softly. Gears wind down to stillness.)*

**[FREEZE FRAME + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]** The factory stands silent. SHIRO and PICO stand back-to-back. Brushstroke text: **"ROOT-CUT — THIRD ROOT"**

Then the factory's machines begin moving again — this time **on their own terms**, in their own proper rhythm. The security guard pushes up his hard hat in delight: "What in the — it's actually running right!"

## Scene 5 | C-Part: Frustration + Ghost Vertical Arc + Pico's First Seed of Growth

**[OUTSIDE THE FACTORY — DAWN]** The sky begins to blush orange. The two are back in chibi mode. PICO slumps against the wall, completely drained.

**PICO:** "…I'm exhausted. Why did that thing keep waking up? Seriously, so stubborn."
**SHIRO:** "Yeah. Pretty stubborn."
**PICO:** "Don't just agree with me! Isn't cutting the power supposed to end it?! Why does it need to put roots down that deep?!"

SHIRO pauses for a moment.

**SHIRO:** "…Hey, Pico. Back in the day — did you ever write a program, think you'd deleted it, and then find out it was still there?"

PICO goes completely still.

**PICO:** "…" *(his eyes waver — like something is surfacing in his memory)*
**PICO:** "…Maybe." *(small. barely a sound.)*

SHIRO doesn't push any further.

**SHIRO:** "**Information is physical. If something put down roots, you have to trace every single one to make it truly gone.** — But Pico — what you did at the end today was good. You drew Daemon in and pulled it away from the roots. Without that, it would've taken a lot longer."
**PICO:** "…I-it's not like I did it for you or anything." *(turns away, but his ears are just a little bit red)*

And then —

**SHIRO:** *(glancing at the factory wall; eyes narrowing)* "…There it is again."
**PICO:** "Huh?"

On the factory wall — a **white heat signature**. Right where SHIRO severed Daemon's roots, an inexplicable **faint pattern of noise** lingers. The same shape as the trace they found in Dubai in Episode 1.

**SHIRO:** *(quietly, almost to himself)* "…Who planted that Daemon? The structure of those roots — **it looks like Ghost's work.**"
**PICO:** "Ghost? As in the Dubai one?"
**SHIRO:** "…Possibly." *(He doesn't say more.)*

SHIRO produces two tickets and lets them flutter.

**SHIRO:** "Let's go, Pico. Next city."
**PICO:** "…Wait. That thing about roots — **I want to actually understand it. Properly.**"

SHIRO turns back, looking almost surprised. PICO glances away, embarrassed.

**PICO:** "…I-it's not a big deal! I just figured knowing more would make me more useful! That's all!"

SHIRO smiles — just barely, just at the corners of his mouth. Quietly.

**SHIRO:** "——Yeah. I'll teach you."

The two walk out into the dawn city.

**[FINAL SHOT]** The heat signature on the factory wall pulses — faintly. In the noise, the faceless silhouette of **Ghost** flickers into view for just a moment — and vanishes. **On-screen text**: *To be continued —*

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

- **Action style**: Root-Cut First/Second/Third — three-strike technique (charge / release / echo) in the Demon Slayer / Naruto tradition. Freeze frame + technique title card. Cooperative move ensures PICO's "action" role has a genuine payoff.
- **Blunder pattern**: Force-shutdown = the anti-pattern of treating symptoms, not causes (distinct from Episode 1's "knock them out one by one"). Daemon multiplying each time it reboots lets the audience *feel* why root removal matters.
- **Real cyber content**: Three-layer persistent malware structure (startup / firmware / hidden partition) + forensic mapping before removal = the Mandiant methodology. No attack how-to; only defensive decision-making shown.
- **Vertical arc**: Foreshadowing — Ghost's trace matches Daemon's root architecture. Pico's "thought I deleted it but didn't" past connects to Episode 1's "I used to be on the other side" line.
- **Growth seed**: Frustration (emotion) → SHIRO's question → "I want to understand" (intellectual curiosity emerging). The blunder isn't minimised, but PICO takes one self-initiated step to help SHIRO at the critical moment — small, but real.
- **7+ safe**: Defeat ≠ destroy (Daemon sleeps, not dies). No blood, no fear, no religious or occult imagery. Daemon's design is neutral-mechanical: gears + scales.
