# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 5: "One Gate. Everyone Gets Checked."

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Cyber Field Guide) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber Topic**: Zero Trust SASE/ZTNA ("Even a familiar face gets checked every single time.")
**Today's Mistake**: "That guy's gotta be friendly" — trusting without verifying (permissive auth with no perimeter)
**Today's Monster**: Impersonation Ghost (mimics "familiar faces" using data particles)

---

## 🎵 OP (10 seconds — same every episode)
Tiny SHIRO & PICO spin and dance in a teal cityscape. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Singapore Smart Port, Before Dawn

**[AERIAL SHOT]** A vast automated container port floating on the sea. Countless cranes move in the amber glow of floodlights — silent, but precise. Lower-third caption: **"Singapore Smart Port — 04:17"**

**[PORT CONTROL TOWER — INTERIOR]** Wall-to-wall monitors stream ship routes, crane utilization rates, and entry-clearance logs. An operator stifles a yawn and stares at the screens.

**OPERATOR** *(muttering to himself)* "Another quiet night…"

— Then.

On one monitor, a **familiar-looking "Port Entry Badge" icon** flickers to life. Green. A legitimate signal.

**OPERATOR** "Oh — must be the regular freight run. Alright, opening the gate—"

*Click.*

**[OUTER PERIMETER FENCE]** A heavy motorized gate rolls slowly open. But instead of a cargo ship — **a translucent cyan-colored "data fog"** drifts through. Softly. Quietly. Seeping into the port.

**[CONTROL TOWER]** Monitors flip one by one — **green → red**. Cranes halt. Containers are stacked in the wrong locations. An alarm sounds — but it's a **gentle, popping blip** (no horror effect).

**OPERATOR** "Wh— why?! The entry badge was legitimate just a second ago—!"

**[ABOVE THE PORT]** Out of nowhere, tiny SHIRO & PICO drop lightly onto a rooftop.

**PICO** *(surveying the port)* "Whoa — every single crane is stopped. The containers are a total mess!"
**SHIRO** *(quietly, eyes narrowing)* "…Someone forged the badge. Something walked in wearing a 'familiar face.'"
**PICO** "Wait — a fake got inside?"
**SHIRO** "Exactly. — **Impersonation.** Today's opponent is going to be tricky."

---

## Scene 2 | The Classic Blunder — Phases ①②③ (Swagger → Slip-Up → Panic)

**[PORT — GATE 1 AREA]** The Ghost's fog spreads slowly through the port. As it drifts closer — **a vague "face" surfaces within it**. SHIRO's face. No — PICO's face. It shifts with every glance. An imitation of someone, built from data particles.

**PICO** *(rolling up his sleeve)* "Okay, leave it to me! For this kind of thing you just find the suspicious one and chase it off, right? I can tell by looking — call it genius instinct!" ← **SWAGGER**

PICO dives into the fog. Data particles swirl and coalesce — taking the shape of **a friend of PICO's**. (A face glimpsed briefly in Episode 1 — one that carries a sense of nostalgia.)

**FOG** *(in the friend's voice)* "Pico~! Over here, over here — you can get through this way!"

**PICO** *(freezing for just a moment)* "Oh… wait, you? …Eh, well — if it's someone I know, it's fine! Come on through, come on through~!" ← **SLIP-UP**

PICO **opens Gate 2 himself** for the mass of fog.

The fog glides through Gate 2 and makes a beeline straight for the heart of the port — **the Container Management System Core**.

**[CONTROL TOWER]** All monitors go dark. Core-intrusion alert.

**PICO** "Huh…? Wait… wait wait wait — it went all the way inside…?"

The moment the fog touches the Core, every crane in the port **begins spinning in reverse simultaneously**. Containers start sliding — slowly, steadily — toward the sea.

**PICO** "Wh— what?! WHY?! I knew its face! I thought it was friendly!" ← **PANIC**

*(Tiny-mode PICO sprints around in circles. Cranes groan and clank. Containers inch toward the water's edge. Top-right corner countdown: "05 / 04 / 03…" flashing red.)*

**PICO** "WHOA, the containers are gonna fall—!"

---

## Scene 3 | SHIRO's Quiet Save + Transformation

**SHIRO** steps silently to PICO's side. Countdown: "02."

SHIRO slowly **extends her right arm wide**. A thin membrane of teal light unfurls to wrap the entire port. The cranes **freeze**. The containers halt at the very edge of the water. *(Wind. Silence. Hair drifting — a moment to breathe.)*

**PICO** "Th— that was amazing… what did you do?"
**SHIRO** *(not turning around, quietly)* "**I closed every gate. All of them.** — Knowing someone's face is not a reason to let them through."
**PICO** "But it *looked* like someone I know…"
**SHIRO** "'Looked like' is not a basis for trust. **You verify. Every single time.** That's — **Zero Trust.**"
**PICO** "Zero… trust…?" *(blank stare)*
**SHIRO** "It doesn't mean 'trust no one from the start.' It means **'verify first, then trust — every time.'** — Explanation later. Right now, we push the Ghost out of the Core. I'm going all out, Pico."
**PICO** *(clenching his fist)* "…This time, I'll do it right."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(shared cut every episode)*
High-five — **"SHIRO-PICO, ON!"** — teal cocoon of light — height upgrade (5-head proportion) — finishing pose (SHIRO: arms wide, deploying the light membrane / PICO: low forward stance, ready to charge).

---

## Scene 4 | Two-Layer Action: Cyberspace + Physical

### ▶ Layer 1: Cyberspace — "Designing the Gate"

Full-height mode. The two sprint into the heart of the port.

Inside is a **visualized network space** — corridors of light crisscross in every direction, each gate appearing as a floating door. The Ghost's fog churns in a spiral around the Core. The fog keeps shifting faces — the control-tower operator, SHIRO, PICO's friend from earlier—

**PICO** "It changed faces again! I can't tell which one is real!"
**SHIRO** "That's exactly why **we treat everyone the same.** Not by face — by **credentials.**"

SHIRO spreads both arms and conjures **a single massive Gate** in the corridor of light.

**SHIRO** "**Single Gate** — every path in this network converges here. No face gets through without passing this point."

The Ghost's fog surges toward the Gate. Once again mimicking PICO's friend's face.

**FOG** *(in the friend's voice)* "Pico, hey Pico—"

**PICO** *(his expression wavers for just a moment — but—)* "…You're not fooling me this time." *(low and steady)*

**SHIRO** *(barely perceptible, glancing sideways)* "…" *(says nothing — but the corner of her mouth lifts, just slightly)*

---

### ▶ Layer 2: Physical — "Take Back the Cranes"

The Ghost's fog tries to force its way through the Gate — **the fog splits into multiple copies** and hammers it all at once.

**SHIRO** "Pico — herd all the copies in front of the Gate. I'll check the credentials."
**PICO** "Copy that—! Technique: **WILD PING — RAPID FIRE!**"

PICO tears through the port at full speed, blasting pulses of light in every direction. Ghost copies are knocked and bounced by the pulses, funneling one after another toward the Gate. *(Leaping across container stacks, using crane arms as platforms — speed lines, *BASH BASH BASH* sound effects.)*

**PICO** "All of them — corralled! Your turn — SHIRO!"

**SHIRO** stands before the Gate. The Ghost copies surge forward, cycling through face after face.

SHIRO closes her eyes.

**SHIRO** *(quietly, drawing the moment out)* "Faces are irrelevant. — **Show me your credentials.**"

Eyes open.

**[TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD: "— ZERO GATE JUDGE"]**

Lattices of teal light radiate from both of SHIRO's hands. The moment the lattice touches the Ghost copies — **anything without a valid credential is simply deflected in a burst of light**. No screams. No pain — just **data particles shifting to teal and drifting softly apart**. (Defeat ≠ destruction = neutralized and dispersed.)

The fog thins. Silence returns to the network corridor.

---

### ▶ Core Liberation

The Ghost's **main body** — a core of cyan fog — makes a break for the deepest part of the Core.

**PICO** "Not getting away—! Technique: **THROUGH-SCAN!**"

PICO drops into a low stance and charges in a straight line. He **traces the outline** of the Ghost's fog core with a line of light.

The core freezes for an instant — having its **contour defined** has locked it in place.

**SHIRO** *(one finger raised, walking forward steadily. A long beat—)* "— **I can see you now.**"

She touches the Core with a single fingertip.

**[TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD: "— GATE CLOSE"]**

Teal light envelops the Core. All the fog transforms into light particles and **drifts softly upward into the sky above the port**.

**[FREEZE FRAME + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]** The two strike their finishing pose against the backdrop of the breaking dawn. Brushstroke lettering: **"ZERO GATE JUDGE."** Orange morning light floods in. Cranes resume normal operation. Containers move to their correct positions.

**OPERATOR** *(in the control tower)* "All systems restored…! What on earth just happened…?"

---

## Scene 5 | C-Part: Ghost's Vertical Arc + Seeds of PICO's Growth

Morning light over the port. Containers are being re-stacked in neat rows. The two — back in tiny mode — sit side by side on a pier.

**PICO** *(knees pulled to his chest, quietly)* "…I got fooled. And I *knew* that face."
**SHIRO** *(sitting beside him, looking out at the sea)* "Ghost uses 'familiarity' as a weapon. It takes whatever you care about and turns it right back on you."
**PICO** "…How did Ghost even *know* that face? How did it know about them?"

SHIRO pauses.

**SHIRO** "…Because data doesn't disappear." *(quietly, gazing into the distance)* "Records of connections. Records of meetings — all of it stays somewhere. Ghost read those records and chose the face you'd be most likely to trust."
**PICO** "…That's creepy." *(rare honesty, no deflection)*
**SHIRO** "Yeah. That's why — **you verify. Every time.**"

Silence. The sound of waves.

**PICO** *(staring off, eyes a little distant)* "…Hey. When I was a kid — when I was being a little punk — I used to exploit people's 'wanting to believe.' Used it to get doors opened for me." *(quietly)* "…That's exactly what Ghost just did."

SHIRO says nothing. She simply shifts a little closer to PICO on the pier.

**PICO** *(looking at his own fist)* "…I never really thought about the people I hurt back then. That scares me. That kind of thing."

**SHIRO** *(softly)* "— A kid who notices that… can change."

PICO turns his face away, embarrassed.

**PICO** "…Wh— what's that about. Getting all serious on me." *(but the corner of his mouth has gone a little soft)*

---

Just then — at the far end of the pier. In the morning mist, **a white heat-shimmer** flickers for just a moment.

**SHIRO** *(eyes narrowing)* "…There's a trace left behind."

Within the shimmer, for just an instant — **the silhouette of a Ghost**. No face. Only the sense that it is **looking back at them**.

**SHIRO** *(low)* "Today's Ghost wasn't just a rogue process. — **Someone was giving it orders.**"

**PICO** "Wait — Ghost has something *above* it?"

**SHIRO** *(without answering, producing two tickets with a light flick of the wrist)* "— Let's go. Next city."

**PICO** "Hey, answer the question! …Ugh, *wait up*—!" *(breaking into a jog)*

**[FINAL SHOT]** In the morning mist, the Ghost's silhouette dissolves into data particles. Those particles drift — slowly, purposefully — **in the direction of a distant city, somewhere far away.**

## 🔎 Cyber Field Guide (30 seconds — channel format)

Back in tiny mode, the two step up to a whiteboard. PICO looks a little sheepish.

**PICO** "So what even *is* this 'zero trust' thing, then?" *(direct to camera)*
**SHIRO** "**Zero Trust = not 'don't trust anyone ever' — it means designing a system where you *verify first, every single time, before you trust.'*** Back in the day, the thinking was 'if you're inside the company network, you're safe' — but now there's no clear perimeter anymore, so *everyone* has to be checked."

A diagram appears on the whiteboard — the line between "INSIDE" and "OUTSIDE" erases itself, replaced by a single path: **"Everyone → Gate → Verify → Pass."**

**PICO** "And that thing we did — funneling everything through one gate — that's what **SASE** and **ZTNA** are?"
**SHIRO** "Exactly! **SASE = a system that bundles networking and security together and delivers it through the cloud. ZTNA = technology that verifies your identity *before* granting access to any app or system, every single time.** Real-world examples include Zscaler, Netskope, and Palo Alto Networks."
**PICO** "…A gate that 'familiar face' alone won't get you through. I'm gonna remember that." *(a little subdued)*
**SHIRO** "— **If you want to learn more, check out 'Yukkuri Cyber Channel'!**" *(PICO flashes a peace sign at the camera — but with a slightly chastened look.)*

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

**Action Craft**: Full deployment of technique names (*Wild Ping Rapid Fire / Through-Scan / Zero Gate Judge / Gate Close*), wind-up, release, resonance, and freeze-frame finish. SHIRO = stillness/single decisive strike (lattice deployment, fingertip touch) vs. PICO = motion/rapid barrage (rapid fire, high-speed run) contrast maintained.

**Blunder Pattern**: Swagger (genius instinct) → Slip-Up (opens gate for face-familiar Ghost) → Panic (containers nearly in the sea) → SHIRO's quiet save (all gates closed) → Lesson (Zero Trust).

**Real Cybersecurity**: Zero Trust philosophy (perimeter dissolution) + SASE/ZTNA (single-point gate) + impersonation attack (social engineering visualized). Zscaler / Netskope / Palo Alto named explicitly in the Field Guide.

**Vertical Arc**: Ghost "receiving orders" = hint of a superior entity, advancing the series mystery. PICO's recognition that "I used to do the same thing" plants the seed of atonement (Episode 9 foreshadowing).

**7+ Safe**: Zero blood, zero fear, zero religious/occult imagery. Ghost is a neutral-design "data particle fog." Defeat ≠ destruction = "particles shift to teal and drift apart" — consistent throughout.

**Two-Layer Structure**: Children = "slapstick of being fooled by a familiar face" / "crane-stoppage tension" / "easy-to-grasp Zero Trust metaphor (the gate)." Adults = "SASE/ZTNA/Zero Trust architecture" / "the real-world challenge of perimeter dissolution" / "the psychological mechanics of impersonation attacks."
