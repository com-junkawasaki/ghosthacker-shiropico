# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 3: "Holes and Beasts Are Different"

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Field Guide) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: Vulnerability Management (Tenable) vs. Threat Intelligence (Mandiant) — "hole-finding tools" and "beast-tracking tools" are not the same thing
**Today's Blunder**: Using a vulnerability scanner meant for finding your own holes to chase an enemy (Daemon) — and missing completely
**This Episode's Monster**: Daemon (main boss) + Ghost's shadow (story arc)
**Story Arc**: Ghost's "scent" appears for the first time — Shiro's expression cracks, just for a moment

---

## 🎵 OP (10 seconds — same every episode)
Chibi Shiro & Pico spin and dance across a teal cityscape. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Dawn at a Singapore Smart Factory

**[AERIAL SHOT]** Singapore at dawn. Enormous container cranes along the harbour glow orange in the morning sun. Behind them — one smart factory, completely dark.

**[FACTORY INTERIOR]** Every robotic arm on the assembly line has **stopped dead**. Display panels flash with rows of gear icons 🔧. Factory staff (silhouettes) rush in every direction.

**STAFF A:** "The line's down! We won't make the morning shipment!"
**STAFF B:** "The computers keep rebooting on their own… it's like something has *moved in*!"

**[DEEP IN THE FACTORY — SHADOWS]** A small figure nestled between the machines. A mechanical sprite with a gear-covered body and a tiny set of scales clutched to its chest — **Daemon**. Its metal-grey frame pulses with blinking orange lights. It looks almost asleep, yet its roots have quietly spread through the entire factory.

**DAEMON** *(low, unhurried voice — a little like a sleepy old uncle)*: "…Tick, tick, tick. The scales… are balanced. I have always been here. Always, always —"

**[FACTORY ROOFTOP]** Chibi Shiro and Pico pop their heads up through a rooftop vent.

**PICO:** "Whoa, the whole factory's frozen! And right before breakfast, too!"
**SHIRO** *(scanning the factory, eyes narrowing)*: "…It's resident. The kind that puts down roots and just… stays."
**PICO:** "Is that the Daemon thing?"
**SHIRO:** "Yeah. **Daemon**. If we leave it alone, it'll take over the whole factory. — Let's move."

---

## Scene 2 | Blunder Pattern ①: The Big Talk

The two drop down into the factory. Pico rolls up his sleeves and digs through his backpack.

**PICO:** "Leave it to me, leave it to me~! I'll scan every last hiding spot and flush this thing right out! Time for a true genius hacker to shine, yeah?" ← ***The Big Talk***

Pico deploys a palm-sized hologram device. Glittering beams of light sweep back and forth across the factory floor.

**PICO:** "**VULNERA-SCAN** — fire!"

Scan light races through every corner of the factory. Small red flags 🚩 pop up all over the place.

**PICO:** "Look at this! The place is full of holes! Everywhere a flag lands — that's where the enemy is! This is gonna be easy~" *(smug grin)*

---

## Scene 3 | Blunder Pattern ②: The Blunder

Pico sprints toward the nearest red flag 🚩 — an old compressor — and smacks it hard.

**PICO:** "Here! Come out!"

…Nothing happens. The compressor sits perfectly still.

Pico runs to the next flag. Smacks a circuit-breaker panel. Nothing.

**PICO:** "Huh…? What about this one…?"

Next flag. Miss. Next flag. Miss.

**[DEEP IN THE FACTORY]** Daemon, its scales blinking calmly, slips into another machine without a care. Every time Pico hits a flag, Daemon simply drifts somewhere else.

**PICO:** "Wait — why can't I land a hit?! There are flags everywhere, but it's nowhere near any of them!?" ← ***The Blunder***

Shiro leans quietly against the wall, watching.

**SHIRO:** "…Pico. What do you think those flags are actually showing you?"
**PICO:** "Where the enemy is! Right?!"
**SHIRO:** "Wrong."

---

## Scene 4 | Blunder Pattern ③: The Panic

**Daemon** slowly expands its territory. The factory lights cut out one by one. It gets dark.

**PICO:** "Wait, it's getting darker!? Oh no — the lines that were stopped are running in reverse now! Everything's moving the wrong way!" ← ***The Panic***

Robotic arms begin swinging in random directions — slow and cartoonish, nobody getting hurt. Pico keeps hitting flags. Daemon is nowhere near any of them. Every flag means another miss.

**PICO:** "Why?! The flags are right there but the enemy isn't! Is my scanner broken?!"
**SHIRO:** "It's not broken. — **You're using the wrong tool.**"

---

## Scene 5 | Shiro's Quiet Save + Transformation

**Shiro** steps over to Pico and gently closes the hologram device.

**SHIRO:** "Pico. What that scanner sees are **'holes'**. Cracks in the walls, unlocked windows — vulnerable spots, like that."
**PICO:** "…Isn't that the same as where the enemy is?"
**SHIRO:** "No. **A hole and the beast that crawled through it are two different things.** You can't track a beast with a hole-finding tool."

Pico stares blankly.

**SHIRO:** "Daemon is already inside. We don't need to know where it got in — we need to know **where it is right now, and what it's doing**. That calls for **Threat Intelligence**: eyes that can read a beast's footprints."
**PICO:** "…Oh! OH! I was using a hole-finding tool to chase a beast! No wonder I couldn't hit anything…!" *(buries face in hands)* "…That's so embarrassing."

**SHIRO** *(the faintest smile)*: "Now you know. That's what matters. — Let's do this for real, Pico."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE]** *(same cut every episode)*
High-five — "**SHIRO-PICO, ON!**" — cocoon of teal light — height upgrade (5-head proportion) — finishing pose (Shiro: one finger raised / Pico: leaning forward, fist clenched).

---

## Scene 6 | Two-Layer Action — First Half: Cyberspace — Reading the Beast's Footprints

Full-height mode. The factory is dark. Daemon's roots glow as orange threads of light, slowly linking machine to machine.

**PICO:** "So how do we track it?"
**SHIRO:** "Every time Daemon moves, it **leaves a trace**. In the logs, in heat signatures, in patterns. — **Information is physical. It doesn't disappear.**"

Shiro closes her eyes. A map of light unfolds in the air — the factory's entire information space. Streams of log data flow like rivers.

**SHIRO:** "Look. Everywhere Daemon has been, **the heat signature is a different colour**. Here… here… and here —"

On the light-map, Daemon's path of movement rises as **orange footprints**. They lead straight toward the heart of the factory — the main control server.

**SHIRO:** "That's where it's trying to take root. **Once it fully embeds in the control server, the whole factory is gone.**"
**PICO:** "We've got a time limit! Let's go!"

**Pico** follows the footprints with his eyes and sprints through the factory, weaving nimbly between robotic arms — acrobatic, but still a little goofy.

**PICO:** "I've got the trail! This way!"

---

## Scene 7 | Two-Layer Action — Second Half: Physical Space — Severing the Roots

**[CONTROL SERVER ROOM]** A dim room. Daemon is wedged snugly between the server racks. Its scales blink in steady rhythm. Roots — orange threads of light — coil around every server in the room.

**DAEMON** *(not turning around, completely unbothered)*: "…Tick, tick. You found me. But too late. I am already part of this place. You cannot pull me out."
**PICO:** "Don't get too comfortable!"

**Pico** charges and yanks at a root — it doesn't budge.

**PICO:** "Ugh, it's solid! It won't come loose!"
**DAEMON:** "The roots are all connected. Pull one out, another grows in its place. I… persist."

**SHIRO** *(stepping quietly forward)*: "**Persistence roots — we sever them all at once.** Pico, pull out every root end you can find. I'll handle the core."
**PICO:** "Every single one…? Do you know how many there are?! — Fine. I'm doing it!"

**[COMBINATION TECHNIQUE — CHARGE]** Pico tears around the room at high speed, grabbing each of Daemon's root ends and yanking them free. The roots spread through the air, forming a glowing web of light. Daemon begins to shudder.

**PICO:** "Got them all! Shiro!"

**Shiro** goes still. Eyes closed. Arms spread wide, taking in the factory's entire information space at once. *(Charge — three seconds of silence. A countdown blinks quietly: 03 / 02 / 01)*

**SHIRO** *(quiet, but certain)*: "The **deepest point** of the roots — that's the only place I need to cut."

She raises one finger, aimed at the scales on Daemon's chest.

**SHIRO:** "— **ROOT CUT.**"

*(Release)* A single flash of teal light. The scales stop with a sharp *clack*. In the next instant — Daemon's roots begin to unravel from the outermost ends inward. Orange light fades away. The servers shift back to teal.

**DAEMON:** "…Tick… tick… w-wait… the scales… won't balance… anymore…"

The scales tilt slowly. Daemon's body comes apart — not vanishing, but **breaking into small gears that tumble gently to the floor** — cute and quiet.

**DAEMON** *(a tiny voice)*: "…I just… wanted to stay here… ZZZ." *(The gears settle, as if falling asleep.)*

*(Beat — silence. The factory lights come back on, one by one, in teal.)*

**[FINISHING STILL + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]** The two heroes strike their finishing pose in the factory's returning light. Brush-stroke title: **"ROOT CUT"**

**PICO:** "…We didn't defeat it — "
**SHIRO:** "We severed the roots and put it to sleep. Daemon is just a tool. Whoever deployed it — that's who we're after."

---

## Scene 8 | C-Part: Ghost's "Scent" — First Appearance

The factory returns to normal. Robotic arms begin moving again, slow and steady. Morning light streams through the windows.

**PICO** *(stretching with relief)*: "Man, that was a workout. But once you cut the roots, everything falls into place — satisfying, right?"

**Shiro** is crouching in front of the sleeping gears (the former Daemon). Staring at them.

**PICO:** "…Shiro? What's wrong?"

**Shiro** touches one of the gears gently with a fingertip. In the gap between gears — **a white heat-signature afterimage** flickers briefly. The same unexplained white trace from Episode 1.

**SHIRO** *(voice shifting, just slightly)*: "…This Daemon. Someone deployed it. — But the way it was set up…"

Shiro stands and projects the afterimage into the air, enlarged. The white heat-trace forms a complex pattern.

**SHIRO:** "…It's the same as **Ghost's signature**."

**PICO:** "Ghost? The one from Dubai?" *(the look of someone remembering Episode 1)*
**SHIRO:** "…Yeah."

For the first time, a shadow crosses Shiro's face. Not her usual half-lidded calm — something deeper, like she's looking at something far away.

**PICO:** "Shiro…?"
**SHIRO** *(snapping back to her normal expression)*: "— I'm taking all the logs from here. They'll be useful."

Pico watches Shiro's profile. Wants to say something. Doesn't.

**PICO** *(quietly)*: "…Hey. Does Ghost have something to do with you?"
**SHIRO:** "…" *(one beat of silence)* "Let's go, Pico. Next city."

Two tickets, flicked into the air. Pico follows — and for just a moment, his face is serious.

**PICO** *(under his breath)*: "…If holes and beasts are different things… then what Shiro's chasing — is the beast."

**[FINAL SHOT]** Outside the factory window, in the morning light — the white heat-signature afterimage surfaces for just a moment, then dissolves. Far away, the faceless silhouette of **Ghost** bleeds into static.

## 🔎 Field Guide Corner (30 seconds — channel format)

Back in chibi form, the two stand at a whiteboard. Pico has drawn a big "HOLE 🕳️" and a big "BEAST 🦊."

**PICO:** "Today's lesson! Holes and beasts are different — that's the one!"
**SHIRO:** "**Vulnerability Management** is a hole-finding tool. Cracks in the wall, unpatched systems — it maps out weak spots. The classic example is **Tenable**. Its job is to **draw the map of holes.**"
**PICO:** "And I used that hole-map to chase the enemy — and missed every single time…"
**SHIRO:** "**Threat Intelligence** is a beast-tracking eye. It reads how a beast moves, what footprints it leaves, where it tries to take root. The classic example is **Mandiant.**"
**PICO:** "So — first you patch the holes (Tenable), and then if something still gets in, you track the beast (Mandiant) — you need **both**?"
**SHIRO:** "Exactly. Neither one is enough on its own."
**PICO** *(turning to camera, thumbs up)*: "**Want to learn more? Head over to 'Yukkuri Cyber Channel'!**" *(peace sign)*

## Production Notes (Self-Assessment)

**Action Grammar**: Technique names (Vulnera-Scan / Root Cut) · charge (3-second countdown) · release (teal flash) · resolution (gears settle to sleep) · finishing still + brush-stroke title card.
**Blunder Pattern**: Big Talk (Vulnera-Scan "this'll be easy" declaration) → Blunder (hits every flag, misses every time, Daemon strolls away) → Panic (lights go out, lines run in reverse) → Shiro's Quiet Save (uses questions to guide Pico to the realisation himself).
**Today's Cyber**: Vulnerability Management (finding holes = Tenable) vs. Threat Intelligence (tracking beasts = Mandiant). "Wrong tool for the job" is the episode's core lesson.
**7+ Safe**: Defeat ≠ destroy (roots severed, Daemon put to sleep, gears tumble down cutely); no blood, no fear, no religious or occult imagery.
**Story Arc**: Ghost's white heat-signature afterimage reappears. Shiro's expression cracks for the first time. Pico realises "Shiro is chasing the beast" — seeds of growth planted.
**Two-Layer Structure**: Kids = slapstick misses → satisfying team combo. Adults = role distinction between vulnerability management and threat intelligence → Field Guide → funnel to Yukkuri Cyber Channel.
