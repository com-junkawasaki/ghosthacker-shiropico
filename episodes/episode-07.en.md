# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 7: "The Key Left in the Sky"

**Runtime**: approx. 11 min (10 min main episode + 1 min Codex Corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: CNAPP (visualizing and fixing cloud misconfigurations)
**Today's Mistake**: Leaving a key (credentials) sitting out in the cloud
**Story Arc**: Mumbai Chapter — Protecting the City / Ghost's shadow draws closer

---

## 🎵 OP (10 seconds — same every episode)
Chibi SHIRO & PICO spin and dance in a teal city. The title logo *SHIRO & PICO* assembles itself from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Mumbai — Before Dawn

**[AERIAL SHOT]** A port city where ocean and skyscrapers collide. The pre-dawn sky bleeds from orange into teal.

The container yard at the docks, a hospital, a railway control room — every system across the city is linked by **cloud-shaped icons (☁️)**, forming a glowing network of light.

**[STILLNESS — SOMETHING'S WRONG]** One point in that network. At the base of a **massive floating cloud island** (a visual metaphor for cloud server space) hanging in the sky, there is a gaping **hole**. Its edges are slowly spreading, and something **dull blue-grey** is seeping out.

**[GROUND LEVEL — THE DOCKS]** A port control terminal operator (silhouette) tilts their head.

**OPERATOR:** "Huh… the container doors are opening on their own—"

Slowly, dock containers begin **opening and closing by themselves**. Railway signals flicker **yellow → red → yellow** in irregular bursts. Hospital electronic record terminals go **completely white**.

**[ROOFTOP]** A breezy rooftop overlooking the sea. Chibi SHIRO stands holding a warm mug of milk, watching the city lights. PICO yawns enormously beside him.

**PICO:** "Mmmgh… is Mumbai breakfast ready yet? I'm *starving*~"

**SHIRO:** *(teal eyes narrowing)* "…Pico. Look at that cloud."

**PICO:** "A cloud? Looks normal— oh. There's a hole in it."

**SHIRO:** *(quiet, but unmistakably tense)* "Everything in the city is leaking out through that hole. — We need to move."

---

## Scene 2 | Mistake Pattern ①: "Big Talk"

**[MID-AIR — NETWORK ENTRY POINT]** The two stand before a gateway of light connecting to the city's network. A ladder of light stretches upward toward the cloud island.

**PICO:** *(rolling up his sleeves, pumping his fist)* "Okay, so there's a hole in the cloud, right? I've dealt with something like this before. **Leave it to me! One shot and I'll patch it — I'm a genius hacker!**" ← **Big Talk**

**SHIRO:** "Wait, hold on—"

**PICO:** *(already running)* "It'll be fine, it'll be fine~! I'll jump in, get to the hole, and—"

PICO sprints up the ladder of light and leaps onto the rim of the cloud island. He slaps a hand into his hip pouch and yanks out a glittering **key-shaped data object** (a visual for authentication credentials).

**PICO:** "Alright — I'll log in with this key and plug the hole directly! Technique: **KEY SLAM!**"

He drives the key into the surface of the cloud island with a *thud*. The hole twitches in response—

**SHIRO:** *(from the bottom of the ladder, eyes narrowing)* "…Pico. Is that key just… stuck in there?"

**PICO:** "Yeah? Keeping it fixed makes it more stable — huh?"

---

## Scene 3 | Mistake Pattern ②: "The Blunder"

A **dull blue-grey haze** begins drifting toward the key embedded in the cloud island's surface. The haze stirs and starts forming a vague outline.

**Eyes** blink open. The **"Cloud Hole"** — this episode's creature — reveals itself.

---

### 👁️ Creature Reveal: **"Cloud Hole"**

> **Design**: A fluffy, drifting mass in dusty blue-grey (#5B7A99) with a gaping hole at its center, slowly inhaling city data (glowing particles) through the gap. Its eyes are two ring-shaped lamps on the hole's rim, glowing a dim orange. Its arms are made of mist — anything they touch gets left "wide open." Far from frightening, it has a sleepy, absentminded quality — but left unchecked, it will drain every last bit of the city's data. When it speaks, its voice is a slow, hollow: *"Fwaaah~…"*

---

**CLOUD HOLE:** "Fwaaah~… open, open. If there's a key… anything can be opened… fwaaah~…"

Using the embedded key, Cloud Hole begins flinging doors open one after another. The port gate, the hospital database, the railway control panel — doors in the information space bang open, and streams of data light pour into the hole. ← **The Blunder**

**PICO:** "Wh— what!? Why is the hole getting *bigger!?* I thought sticking the key in would lock it down—"

**SHIRO:** *(climbing the ladder, calm)* "Because you left the key in, *anyone* can use it now. The hole got to the key before you did."

**PICO:** "But — isn't having the key there more convenient!?"

**SHIRO:** "Convenient and safe are **two different things**."

---

## Scene 4 | Mistake Pattern ③: "The Panic"

The Cloud Hole keeps growing. A network map projected in mid-air shows connected systems turning **red** one by one. Docks → Railway → Hospital → Power Grid…

**PICO:** *(chibi mode, running in frantic circles)* "Nooo, it's spreading, it's spreading! Not just the docks — the *hospital* too!? This is bad, what do we do, SHIRO?! Do something about my key!!" ← **The Panic**

Cloud Hole drifts lazily toward PICO. It extends a misty arm and *pops* open PICO's hip pouch.

**PICO:** "Hey! Don't open my pouch!"

**CLOUD HOLE:** "Fwaaah~… open is easier… open is nicer…"

**PICO:** *(clutching the pouch shut)* "It is NOT nicer!"

**SHIRO:** *(stepping quietly in front of PICO, facing the creature)* "— Pico. Step back."

---

## Scene 5 | SHIRO's Quiet Countermove + Transformation Sequence

SHIRO faces Cloud Hole directly. Eyes narrow.

**SHIRO:** "You left the key in and walked away. — A **'key left behind'** is the most dangerous thing there is. First, pull the key out. Make the hole **visible**. That's all we need to do."

He smoothly pulls the key free. Cloud Hole lets out a startled *"Fwaaah!?"* and shrinks slightly.

**PICO:** "…That's it? But the hole's still there."

**SHIRO:** "The hole was always there. **No one was looking at it.** — Once you can see it, you can fix it."

*(A beat. SHIRO glances over at PICO. Quietly, gently.)* "— Let's do this for real, Pico."

**PICO:** *(a face caught between frustration and excitement)* "…Fine. This time I'm watching."

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE — 3.5 seconds, 5 cuts, same every episode]**
High-five → **"SHIRO-PICO, ON!"** → Cocoon of teal light → Height upgrade (5-head proportion) → Pose lock (SHIRO: one finger raised, white hoodie hood billowing open / PICO: forward lean, lime-green eyes flashing).

---

## Scene 6 | Action — First Half: CNAPP — "See Everything at Once"

Full-height mode. Cloud Hole floats in the air above them, reaching misty arms toward system after system.

**PICO:** "It's getting huge! Where do we even start!?"

**SHIRO:** *(closing his eyes, white hoodie trailing in a teal wind)* "We see every hole at once. — That's **CNAPP**."

**[INFORMATION SPACE — OVERHEAD VIEW]** SHIRO's awareness expands. The entire cloud island connected to the city unfolds as a map of light. Docks, hospital, railway, power grid — the status of every "door" appears in a single display.

- 🔴 Dock Control Gate: **Wide open**
- 🔴 Hospital Database: **No key**
- 🟡 Railway Control Panel: **Key is outdated**
- 🟢 Power Grid: **Normal**

**SHIRO:** "Every door's status — visible at a glance. — The red ones are the immediate threats."

**PICO:** *(staring at the map)* "Whoa, there's so much red… were all of these holes open this whole time?"

**SHIRO:** "They weren't opened. **Nobody noticed they were open.** CNAPP is a tool that makes **all of that visible**."

**PICO:** *(going quiet for a moment, expression turning serious)* "…Is that what I did, too? Leaving my key behind?"

**SHIRO:** *(a quiet nod)* "Exactly that."

---

## Scene 7 | Action — Middle: PICO's Rapid Strikes and SHIRO's One Move

Cloud Hole stretches its misty arms toward the red doors again.

**PICO:** "Alright — I'll shut every red door before it gets there!"

**SHIRO:** "Pico — **prioritize**—"

**PICO:** "Got it! Hospital is the most critical, then the docks!"

PICO launches into motion. Kicking off platforms of light, he races across the cloud island and snaps **temporary padlocks (patches)** onto red doors in rapid succession.

**Technique — Rapid Fire**: "**DOOR STAMP! DOOR STAMP! DOOR STAMP!**"

Hospital door — *click*. Dock door — *click*. Railway door — *click*.

**CLOUD HOLE:** "Fwaaah~… closing… closing…" *(shrinks slightly, looking confused)*

**PICO:** *(catching his breath)* "Yes! Every red door — locked!"

**SHIRO:** "Good work. — But we're not done."

**PICO:** "Huh?"

**SHIRO:** "Temporary padlocks **will open again**. We have to fix the **root** — the original misconfiguration."

SHIRO points at the map. The center of the cloud island. A cluster of configuration files — a cube of light — with a small **crack** running through it.

**SHIRO:** "There. The **root of the settings**."

---

## Scene 8 | Action — Final Half: Combined Technique "CLOUD CLEAR"

Cloud Hole makes its last stand. It throws its misty arms wide and wraps the two of them in a **wall of fog**. Visibility drops to zero.

**PICO:** "I can't see anything!"

**SHIRO:** *(eyes still closed, calm)* "Even without sight, **the logs are still there**. Data is physical — it doesn't disappear."

Light particles gather in SHIRO's palm. The **white heat traces** left behind by Cloud Hole — every "opening" it ever made — rise as lines of light.

**SHIRO:** "— I can **see it**." *(quiet, certain)*

**PICO:** *(eyes lighting up as the glow reaches him)* "…I can see it too! It's over there!"

Together, they sprint through the fog without hesitation — straight to the configuration cube.

**[THE CHARGE-UP]** They stand facing each other on either side of the cube. SHIRO raises his right hand. PICO raises his left. Both aimed at the crack.

**[THE RELEASE]**
**SHIRO:** "Set the configuration — **back to what it should be**."
**PICO:** "Watch how I work!"

**Both together:** "— **CLOUD CLEAR!!**"

Two beams of light pour into the crack. The configuration file rewrites itself to its correct state — a cascade of glowing code flows across the cube.

**[THE AFTERMATH]** Cloud Hole lets out a slow *"Fwaaah~…"* and begins to shrink. The fog lifts. The hole contracts — slowly, steadily — until it becomes a small, **fluffy cloud puff**. It blinks its sleepy eyes once, twice—

**CLOUD HOLE:** "Fwaaah… settings… fixed… fwah~… *(snooze)*"

It curls into a ball and **drifts quietly to sleep** at the edge of the cloud island. (Not defeated — *restored*. Correct settings bring it to rest.)

**[POSE LOCK + TECHNIQUE TITLE CARD]** The two hold their finishing pose against a clear cloud island sky. The brushstroke title card reads: **"CLOUD CLEAR"**

The city network map goes **all green**. Docks, hospital, railway — every system: normal.

---

## Scene 9 | C-Part: Ghost's Shadow and the Seed of PICO's Growth

On top of the cloud island. Both back to chibi form. The Mumbai sunrise is beginning — the sea glows orange.

**PICO:** *(gazing down at the city)* "…The hospital's running fine. Good."

**SHIRO:** "Yeah."

**PICO:** *(after a pause)* "Hey… I used to leave 'keys behind' in the cloud on purpose, you know. So I could get back in later."

**SHIRO:** *(silent, listening)*

**PICO:** "Back then, I never thought about hospitals being connected to any of it. — Never thought about anyone getting hurt."

*(Looking out at the sea. Lime-green eyes, distant.)*

**PICO:** "…When I saw the hospital door turn red today, I felt… something. Like it hurt. In my chest."

**SHIRO:** *(slowly)* "That means you **understand** now."

**PICO:** "…Still annoyed about it, though."

**SHIRO:** *(a small smile)* "Being annoyed about it is exactly right."

---

**[WIDE STILL SHOT]** At the edge of the cloud island, beside the sleeping Cloud Hole, a **white heat trace** flickers for just a moment.

**SHIRO:** *(eyes narrowing)* "…There's another trace here."

**PICO:** "Ghost?"

**SHIRO:** "…This hole wasn't open from the start. — Someone **opened it**."

SHIRO reviews the configuration error log left by Cloud Hole. The lines of light converge on a single point — **coordinates for a distant, unknown location**.

**SHIRO:** *(quiet, but with unmistakable tension)* "Ghost is **using the cloud as a stepping stone**."

**PICO:** "What does that mean?"

**SHIRO:** "The hole wasn't an accident. — Someone was **made** to create the misconfiguration. Deliberately."

PICO's breath catches.

**[FINAL SHOT]** Far away in the sky, the faceless silhouette of **Ghost** dissolves into static and vanishes. In its hand, something shaped like a glowing **key**.

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

**Action grammar**: Technique names (KEY SLAM / DOOR STAMP ×3 rapid-fire / CLOUD CLEAR) · charge-up (facing each other at the cube) · release (simultaneous) · aftermath (Cloud Hole snoozes) · pose lock + technique title card = *Demon Slayer / Naruto*-style structure.

**Mistake pattern**: ①Big Talk (leave it to me, genius) → ②The Blunder (key left in → creature uses the key) → ③The Panic (nooo it's spreading) → ④SHIRO's quiet countermove (pull the key out + the lesson).

**Real-world cyber**: CNAPP (full cloud configuration visibility) + credential exposure (most common cloud mistake) + prioritized remediation = the actual professional workflow, rendered naturally. Real tools (Wiz, Prisma Cloud, Orca) kept to the Codex Corner.

**Defeat ≠ destroy**: Cloud Hole simply falls asleep once the settings are corrected. Zero bloodshed, zero fear, zero religious or occult imagery.

**Story arc**: Ghost was using the cloud as a C2 stepping stone — not a standalone monster-of-the-week, but a piece of Ghost's larger plan. PICO's past (deliberate credential abandonment) → chest pain at seeing the hospital door turn red = a clear midpoint on his "power to steal → power to protect" character arc.

**Two-layer structure**: Kids = fluffy cloud island, fog wall, sleepy landing gag. Adults = CNAPP visualization, credential leakage, root-cause misconfiguration fix, C2 pivot implication.
