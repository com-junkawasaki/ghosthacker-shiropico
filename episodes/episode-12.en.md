# "SHIRO & PICO" Episode 12: "The Power to Protect"

**Runtime**: approx. 11 min (10 min main + 1 min Codex Corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: NIST CSF maturity (the five stages: Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover) + all-out battle
**Today's Oops**: trying to do it all alone and spinning out → relying on your friends (the grand finale)
**Story Arc**: First Ghost showdown / Pico becomes an equal / the final sweep

---

## 🎵 OP (10 sec, same every episode)
In the teal city, chibi SHIRO & PICO spin and dance. The title logo "SHIRO & PICO" assembles from data particles.

---

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Simultaneous Worldwide — A Storm of Heat Traces

**【Pull back from space】** Earth at night. Cities glowing teal in a web — Tokyo, Dubai, Mumbai, Chicago.
Peace — or so it seems, until **that light all at once begins to stain the color of noise**.

**【Tokyo intersection】** Every traffic light goes dark. Countless automatic doors slam open and shut, over and over.
**【Mumbai hospital】** Electronic chart terminals nod off one after another — **bonk… ZZZ** (the same gag as the sleepy car).
**【Dubai port】** Container cranes start moving on their own, swinging cargo around and around.

**Narration (SHIRO's voice, quiet)**
"Information is physical. Even when you think you've erased it — a trace always remains."

**【Cyberspace, overhead view】** On the map of light, three colors of storm swirl: red, orange, blue.
👻 Ghost's cyan particles, 😈 Daemon's gears, 🧟 Zombie's Zzz marks — **all three are deploying at once, on a global scale.**

**【Top floor of an abandoned building, the "edge" of cyberspace】** The faceless silhouette of Ghost wavers within the noise.
No voice — it just raises a single finger.

**Countdown "05 / 04 / 03…"** (blinking red. Suspense, no words needed.)

---

## Scene 2 | The Oops Pattern ①–③ (Cocky → Oops → Panic)

**【Rooftop of abandoned building, ground level】** Chibi SHIRO & PICO leap down and land.
**SHIRO** (slowly scanning the whole city) "…It's all moving at once. Ghost is bundling all three types together. This is the biggest one yet."
**PICO** (rolling up his sleeves, eyes sparkling) "But today we end all of it. — Leave it to me!" ← **Cocky**

PICO plants his hands on the ground and rattles off every technique he's learned, one after another.

**PICO** "Zombie first! — **WILD WAKE-UP!**"
WHAM — a pulse of light. A few nearby sleeping terminals go "Whoaaa!" and wake up.
**PICO** "Next, Daemon! — **ROOT CUT!**"
SNAP — one of the gear Daemons clunks to a stop.
**PICO** "Found Ghost too! — **HEAT-TRACE!**"
ZWIP — he dashes off, chasing a white heat afterimage.

…But.

The Zombies he woke up **pass their "drowsiness" to the terminals next door** (déjà vu from Episode 1).
The Daemon's root wasn't cut, so it **auto-restarts again** — "clunk, clunk…"
And Ghost's heat trace **split into three directions** — the one PICO chased was **a fake**. A total whiff. ← **Oops**

**PICO** "Wha— huh?! The Zombies are multiplying again! And the Daemon's back too! Where's Ghost?!"
(In chibi mode he scrambles in three directions. Sound effects everywhere. Counter hits "02.")
**PICO** "If I try to do it all alone… I can't keep up with any of it! Wahhh!" ← **Panic**

---

## Scene 3 | SHIRO's Quiet Follow + Transformation Bank

**SHIRO** doesn't move. Eyes narrowed, watching PICO.

**SHIRO** "— Pico."
**PICO** (still running around) "I know, I know! Just a little more and—!"
**SHIRO** (one step forward. Quiet, but clear) "— You're spinning out because you're carrying it all alone."

PICO's feet stop.

**SHIRO** "Zombie, Daemon, Ghost. When all three come at once, **trying to smash them all yourself is symptom-treatment taken to the extreme.** — Pico, remember what I taught you? **Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.** At which stage, who moves?"

PICO takes a breath. — Slowly, his face remembering.

**PICO** "…………Sort them out, split the roles, move in order. Don't try to do everything alone, huh."
**SHIRO** "Right. — Today's enemy is big. So today, **we go all out.**"

PICO holds out his hand. SHIRO — for the first time — gives a small smile.

**🎵【Transformation Bank】** (same cut every episode)
High five → "**SHIRO-PICO, ON!**" → teal cocoon of light → scale up (5-head height) → finishing pose (SHIRO: one finger poised / PICO: leaning forward).

A one-time-only added cut — the cocoon's light is **a notch brighter than usual**, and the two cast long shadows.

---

## Scene 4 | Action Part 1: The All-Out Battle of NIST CSF's Five Stages

The two in full-size mode. The three-color storm covers the whole city.

**PICO** "Tell me the plan." (this time not "leave it to me" but "tell me")
**SHIRO** "**We move in NIST CSF order.** — First, **Identify.** See what the enemy is after."

SHIRO closes her eyes. Logs from across the city become lines of light, gathering in the air (SIEM effect).

**SHIRO** "Ghost is bundling them. With Zombie it 'puts terminals to sleep,' with Daemon it 'roots the auto-restart' — and in that gap, Ghost is **peeking at every single log.** What it's after is **the information itself.**"
**PICO** "Peeking at the logs…! Then next is **Protect.** Put up a wall to guard them before they're stolen!"

PICO springs forward — this time not to smash on his own, but **running to raise a wall.**

**PICO** "**BARRIER FALL!**" (Technique name — charge: leaning stance — release: slams both hands to the ground, a teal membrane of light unfurls across the streets)
Ghost's gaze (tendrils of cyan particles) bounces off the membrane. "…ngh" (Ghost retreats slightly for the first time.)

**SHIRO** "Next, **Detect.** — Where is the Daemon's root?"
SHIRO reels in the bundle of log-light. One orange line stretches deep underground.
**SHIRO** "An old server underground. That's the 'root.'"

---

## Scene 5 | Action Part 2: Cut the Daemon's Root, Wake the Zombies

**【Underground, old server room (cyberspace)】**
A gear Daemon reaching to the ceiling repeats its auto-restart, "clunk, clunk." A giant version — the biggest this season.

**PICO** "It's huge! …But I know how. **Respond** — just cut the root."
**SHIRO** "I'll go with you."

The two, back to back. A storm of Daemon gears rains down.

**PICO** (parrying gears in rapid, high-speed bursts) "Shiro, I'll open the path to the root! Now!"
**SHIRO** (advancing quietly. Threading the gaps between gears in the fewest moves) "— **ROOT CUT, DEEP LAYER.**"
With a single finger, she touches the deepest core of the underground server's "root."

**Daemon** "Clunk… clunk… cl—…" (the gears stop one by one and go still) "Cluuunk… fwah" (cutely going limp and falling asleep).

**SHIRO** "Root cut. Next is Zombie. — It's **Recover** now, Pico."
**PICO** "'Wake them up gently' — got it!"

PICO stands before the sleeping terminals. This time, not smashing one by one — **he takes in all of them at once, isolates them, then wakes them** (applying the EDR he learned in Episode 1).

**PICO** "**SLEEP-CALL, FULL DEPLOY!**" (charge — a big breath in / release — arms spread wide, a teal ripple goes in all directions / afterglow — the ripple slowly wraps around every terminal)
The sleeping terminals come to their senses one after another, "Whoaaa! Mornin'!" **Not a single one missed.**

**PICO** (smirking) "…I've gotten a little better than Episode 1, huh, me."

---

## Scene 6 | Action Part 3: First Ghost Showdown / The Final Sweep

Zombie and Daemon have gone quiet — but the world's noise still hasn't faded.
**Ghost's cyan particles condense and begin to take form on the top floor of the abandoned building.**

**SHIRO** (eyes narrowing, voice shifting slightly) "…It's here."
**PICO** "Shiro?"
**SHIRO** "— It's Ghost. This season, **its true body has finally appeared.**"

Ghost's outline wavers with noise. No face. No voice. Just — cyan particles swirling, **trying to swallow every log in the city** (its final move to "steal" information).

**PICO** "It's gonna take all the logs!"
**SHIRO** "— Information is physical. **Even erased, the trace remains.** Ghost, your heat trace — I've seen it for a long, long time."

Ghost's motion stops for the first time.

**SHIRO** (quiet, but certain) "— I can already see you." (signature line)

Ghost wavers for an instant — just for an instant, its outline edges toward a "human shape." SHIRO's eyes narrow (an old grudge — no words, but it carries through).

**PICO** (seeing it, lining up beside SHIRO. Quietly) "…Shiro, don't panic. Information doesn't run away, right?"

SHIRO pauses a beat. — And smiles, a little.

**Both step forward at once.**

**PICO** "Don't underestimate my touch. — Now it's for my friends." (his post-growth signature line)
**SHIRO** "— **HEAT-TRACE, FULL ANALYSIS.**" (charge — eyes closed, converging every log in the city / release — white light from her fingertip fully illuminates Ghost's heat trace / afterglow — Ghost's outline surfaces in teal)

**PICO** "Found it—! — **FULL BREAK!**" (charge — leaning forward at a full sprint / release — both hands touch the "core" of Ghost's outline / afterglow — cyan particles scatter, turning to white heat-light)

**【Combo Technique — Finishing Freeze + Technique-Name Title Card】**
"— **DOUBLE HEAT BREAK!**"
The two in a finishing pose against the night skyline. Brush-stroke title card.

Ghost — **doesn't vanish.** The core is shattered, but its silhouette, dispersing into mist, **retreats** beyond the noise.

**Ghost** (for the first time, a voice. Quiet and low, gender unknown) "…You two still know nothing."

And then — it's gone.

**PICO** "It got away?!"
**SHIRO** "…It didn't run. **It withdrew.** — Today, this is as far as it goes."
**PICO** "We couldn't beat it…"
**SHIRO** "We couldn't beat it. But — **Ghost retreated for the first time.** And that's because of everything we've built up to today."

## Scene 7 | Part C: Pulling the Ghost Arc Forward + Planting the Seed of Pico's Growth

The city has fallen quiet. The world's light slowly returns to teal.

Back in chibi mode, the two sit on the abandoned building's rooftop, watching the night view.

**PICO** "…I tried to do everything alone and spun out, at first."
**SHIRO** "Yeah."
**PICO** "But once I sorted out the roles and moved in order — it kinda… actually worked."
**SHIRO** "**NIST CSF.** Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. You stack them in order, so it doesn't collapse. — **That's what maturity means.**"
**PICO** "…'Maturity,' huh. I'm still nowhere close, though."
**SHIRO** (quietly) "Today, for the first time — you found Ghost's core before I did."
**PICO** "Huh?"
**SHIRO** "I was hesitating. If you hadn't spoken up, I'd have been a moment late."

PICO scratches his head, embarrassed.

**PICO** "…That's kinda unfair. Saying stuff like that."

Silence. The night breeze.

**SHIRO** (softly) "— Did you hear Ghost's voice?"
**PICO** "The 'still know nothing' thing? What was that about?"
**SHIRO** (eyes narrowing) "…I heard it. That voice — **it's a voice I know.**"

PICO looks at SHIRO's profile. SHIRO says nothing more.

**PICO** "…Will you tell me someday?"
**SHIRO** "— Yeah. Someday, for sure."

Two tickets flutter down.

**SHIRO** "Let's go, Pico. — Ghost isn't finished yet."
**PICO** "…Tch, I'm not letting it get away next time!" (trotting after her)

**【Final Cut】** Far off in the night sky, Ghost's white heat afterimage faintly wavers. For an instant, its outline looks like — **a human shape.**

**Title card**: The showdown with Ghost continues —

## 🔎 Codex Corner (30 sec, channel-style)

Back in chibi mode, the two stand before a whiteboard. On the board, "NIST CSF" is written big.

**PICO** "So what's this 'nist-cee-ess-eff' thing today, huh?"
**SHIRO** "**NIST CSF — the National Institute of Standards and Technology's Cybersecurity Framework.** It organizes security into **five stages.**"

SHIRO draws five circles on the board.

**SHIRO** "**Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover.** Stack them in this order, and no matter what attack comes, it won't collapse. — **Maturity** is the level of how well you can actually do these five stages."
**PICO** "So even if three types come at once like today, if you move in order you won't spin out~!"
**SHIRO** "Right. — With real tools, you collect and detect logs with **Splunk or Microsoft Sentinel** (SIEM), you isolate and respond to terminals with **CrowdStrike or SentinelOne** (EDR), and you cut the root with **Mandiant** (forensics). You split the roles and make them work together."
**PICO** "It was all connected — everything I've been learning!"
**SHIRO** "**Kids who want to know more, head to the 'Yukkuri Cyber ch,' okay!**" (PICO flashes a peace sign at the camera.)

## Design Notes (self-review)

**Action craft**: Technique names (Wild Wake-Up / Barrier Fall / Root Cut Deep Layer / Sleep-Call Full Deploy / Heat-Trace Full Analysis / Full Break / Double Heat Break), charge, release, afterglow, and finishing freeze implemented on every technique.

**Oops pattern, grand finale**: the early "trying to do it all alone and spinning out" is an upgraded version of Episode 1's "smashing one terminal at a time." Same structure, new substance. Growth = the shrinking of the oops, completed in the final episode.

**The final sweep**: SHIRO's momentary wobble at Ghost's voice → PICO flips it with "Don't panic. Information doesn't run away, right?" (the bible's Chapter 9 final-sweep line fully implemented).

**NIST CSF implementation**: the five stages — Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover — are tied directly to the action staging, and the Codex Corner makes the link to previously learned tools (SIEM / EDR / forensics) explicit. The capstone of the 12-episode curriculum.

**Story arc**: Ghost's first voice / first retreat / the "voice I know" foreshadowing / Ghost's outline as a human shape = hints of a direct link to SHIRO's past. 7+ safe (defeat ≠ rescue; no blood, no fear, no religious/occult motifs).
