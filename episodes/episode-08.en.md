# *SHIRO & PICO* — Episode 8: "The Invisible Map"

**Runtime**: Approx. 11 min (10 min main episode + 1 min Field Guide corner) / **Rating**: 7+ / TV-Y7-FV
**Today's Cyber**: Reconnaissance — Nmap (self-diagnosis from the defender's side)
**Today's Mistake**: Trying to use an offensive skill offensively
**Character Arc**: Pico learns what power is actually *for*

---

## 🎵 OP (10 seconds — same every episode)
Chibi Shiro & Pico spin and dance in a teal city. The title logo **SHIRO & PICO** assembles itself from data particles.

## Scene 1 | Cold Open: Singapore — Port, Before Dawn

**[AERIAL SHOT]** Singapore before dawn. A massive container port, towers of freight stacked to the sky. Cranes stand in perfect rows; neon and searchlights shimmer across the water. *A peaceful night of logistics — or so it seems.*

Inside the port's control building, a wall of giant monitors tells a different story. The icons for the container-tracking system, the entry-and-exit gates, the freezer-warehouse temperature sensors — one by one, every terminal icon is turning **grey**. It's as though someone is erasing the map with a rubber.

**OPERATOR** *(panicking)*: "What's happening?! The sensors — I can't *see* them anymore!"

**[OFFSHORE]** Out on the dark water, a swarm of semi-transparent cyan particles glides silently toward the port, skimming the surface like grains of sand. Wherever they touch a terminal, its "eyes" close — quietly, one by one. Not frightening. Just... *gone*.

**[PORT ENTRANCE — GROUND LEVEL]** Chibi Shiro and Pico walk in carrying their bags.

**PICO**: "Singapore! I've always wanted to come here! I want that amazing chicken rice!"
**SHIRO** *(stopping, eyes narrowing)*: "...Pico. The control building's logs have gone dark."
**PICO**: "Huh? Work before food?"
**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "The port has gone *invisible*. — Someone is painting over the map."

Pico stares at the control building monitors. The grey blankness spreads, slowly and steadily.

**PICO** *(expression going serious, just a little)*: "...That's creepy."

**[TITLE CARD]**: *Episode 8 — "The Invisible Map"*

---

## Scene 2 | Mistake Pattern ①: Showboating

Inside the control building. Operators rush around in a panic. Shiro and Pico enter.

**OPERATOR**: "Who are you two...?"
**SHIRO**: "White-hat hackers. Tell us the situation."
**OPERATOR**: "Two hours ago, sensors and gate terminals all over the port started going dark, one after another. We don't know where any container is, when any ship is arriving — we can't see *anything* anymore!"

Shiro closes their eyes and thinks for a moment.

**SHIRO**: "...We're being reconnoitred. Someone is scanning this entire port network, hunting for weak spots. — First, we need to rebuild our own map."
**PICO** *(eyes lighting up, rolling up a sleeve)*: "Recon? I'm *great* at that! It's exactly what I used to do all the time! Leave it to me — genius on the job!" ← **Showboating**

---

## Scene 3 | Mistake Pattern ②: The Blunder

Pico drops to one knee, spreads both arms wide, and starts firing pulses of light.

**PICO**: "Technique — **FULL-SCAN BLAST!!**"

*FLASH-FLASH-FLASH!* Waves of scanning light scatter across the entire port — indiscriminately.

A moment of silence.

...Then every terminal in the port **erupts in alarms at once**.

**ALARM**: "⚠️ Suspicious scan detected — severing connection — ⚠️"

Even the terminals that were still working **mistake Pico's scan for an attack and auto-shut-down**. The map that was grey before is now **solid black**. ← **The Blunder**

**OPERATOR**: "W-why?! Even the ones that were still running!!"

---

## Scene 4 | Mistake Pattern ③: Panic

**PICO**: "Wha — hold on, WAIT?! Why are they dropping?! I was *trying to help*!!" ← **Panic**

Pico scrambles around in a frenzy. Chibi-mode sound effects: *THUMP THUMP THUMP.*

**PICO**: "I'm sorry! Wake up! Please wake up, little terminals!!"

Pico pats the screens frantically. Nothing happens, of course.

**OPERATOR** *(head in hands)*: "The port is... completely..."

**PICO** *(going pale, turning to Shiro)*: "...Shiro... did I just make everything worse?"

---

## Scene 5 | Shiro's Quiet Save

Shiro lets out one slow breath. No scolding. Shiro crouches quietly beside Pico and looks at the monitors.

**SHIRO**: "...Pico. That technique — what did you used to use it for?"
**PICO** *(sheepish)*: "...To find holes in a target's system before I broke in, I guess."
**SHIRO**: "Right. **Studying your target, finding the weak spots — that's reconnaissance**. — But defenders have the same technique."
**PICO**: "...Defenders do?"
**SHIRO**: "**You scan your own system yourself, and you find the weak spots first** — before the attacker does. You build the map before they can. — That's how **Nmap** is supposed to work. But the *way* you ran it was wrong."

Shiro stands up.

**SHIRO**: "When you fire it indiscriminately, the defensive systems read it as an *attack*. You have to **narrow the scope, run it quietly, run it carefully**. — Same power. Different approach. And a different approach can destroy the very thing you're trying to protect."

Pico bites their lip.

**PICO**: "...So I was basically doing the same thing as attacking?"
**SHIRO** *(glancing at Pico, quietly)*: "You realised it now. That's what matters. — Let's go again."

---

## Scene 6 | Transformation Sequence

Shiro holds out a hand. Pico hesitates for just a second — then *slap*, a high-five.

**BOTH TOGETHER**: "**SHIRO-PICO, ON!**"

**🎵 [TRANSFORMATION BANK]** *(same cut every episode)*
A cocoon of teal light — full height (5-head scale) — finishing pose.
Shiro: one finger raised in quiet focus. Pico: forward-leaning stance — but today, the eyes are just a little more careful.

## Scene 7 | Action — First Half: Cyberspace — The Self-Diagnosis Map

Full-height mode. The port's network space stretches out as a dark three-dimensional maze. Countless nodes sit sunken in grey. And threading through the walls of the maze — **swarms of cyan particles**, sliding silently. Creatures — **Ghost's scouts**.

Small. Almost cute. But wherever they touch a node, its "eyes" close.

**PICO**: "They're so *quiet*. I can't tell where any of them are."
**SHIRO**: "That's why *we* build our map first. — Pico, let's do it together this time. Set the scope, run it quietly."

Shiro spreads both hands, drawing a **grid of light** across the space. The outline of the port network rises slowly into view.

**SHIRO**: "— **Self-Mapping.**"

**PICO** *(standing beside Shiro, carefully releasing small, gentle pulses)*: "...Like this?"
**SHIRO**: "Exactly. **Quiet. Careful.**"

The grid of light sweeps across the nodes, one by one. Healthy nodes light up **teal**. Compromised nodes — the ones the scouts have touched — **blink orange**.

**PICO**: "Oh... I can see it. The map is... taking shape."

**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "— **Nmap**. Scan your own network yourself. See the full picture of what it looks like *right now*. Build the map before the attacker can."

The map completes itself. The full outline of the port network — healthy nodes, compromised nodes, and one single point where all the scouts are converging — **pulsing red**.

**PICO**: "That spot... is that the source?"
**SHIRO**: "The scouts' rally point. — Cut that off, and the map comes back. Let's go."

---

## Scene 8 | Action — Second Half: Physical Space — Chasing the Scouts, Severing the Rally Point

**[PORT — PHYSICAL SPACE]** Dawn is close. Between the containers, swarms of cyan particles flow and slide. The rally point — an **old wireless relay tower** at the edge of the port. Rust-eaten steel girders, swarming with particles that spiral and eddy around them.

**PICO**: "That relay tower! All right, I'm going straight in!"
**SHIRO**: "Wait. — Scatter the scouts first. Pico, can you draw them out?"
**PICO** *(grinning)*: "Now *that* I can do. — This is my specialty."

Pico sprints across the tops of the containers, scattering fake light pulses in every direction.

**PICO**: "Over here! Come and get me! This way!"

The scouts swarm toward Pico, the particles stretching out in a long line.

**PICO**: "Shiro! Now!"

Shiro moves to the base of the relay tower. Approaches quietly. Eyes close.

*Charging—*

Teal light condenses at Shiro's fingertip. Small, but impossibly dense.

**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "— **Root Silence.**"

*Release—*

A single thread of light from the fingertip — straight into the tower's "root."

*Stillness—*

The cyan particles coating the relay tower pop and crackle — then **drift apart and dissolve, soft as soap bubbles**. Not scary at all. The scouts blink their round little eyes, spin in confused circles, and come to a gentle wobbling stop.

**SCOUTS**: "...Fnyah?" *(lost-puppy faces)*

**PICO**: "Hey — they stopped!"

Shiro steps up to one of the halted scouts and places a hand gently on it.

**SHIRO**: "— **Data Capture**. I'll take the trail you left behind."

The scout dissolves into soft particles and flows into Shiro's palm. Shiro watches it intently.

**SHIRO** *(quietly, almost to themselves)*: "...Just as I thought. The way these scouts move — it's close to Ghost's core signature."

---

## Scene 9 | Resolution — Aftermath

Back in the port's control building. The grey on the monitors fades away, node by node, replaced by **teal**.

**OPERATOR**: "The sensors — they're coming back! The gates too! The map — I can see *everything* again!"

The staff break into cheers. Outside the windows, the first light of dawn washes across the port. The container cranes begin to move, slow and steady.

Pico stands at the window, watching the port, and speaks quietly.

**PICO**: "...You know, I used to do this kind of scanning all the time. To break into other people's systems. — It's weird. It's the same technique, but it feels completely different."
**SHIRO** *(standing beside Pico)*: "What's different about it?"
**PICO** *(thinking for a moment)*: "...The feeling when the map finished. It felt good in a different direction, I guess. Back then, finding the hole felt like — *gotcha*. But today... it was something else."

Shiro says nothing. But the corner of their mouth lifts, just slightly.

**PICO**: "...Why are you smiling?"
**SHIRO**: "I'm not smiling."
**PICO**: "You totally were!!"

---

## Scene 10 | C-Part: Ghost's Arc + Seeds of Growth

At the far edge of the port. Shiro holds the scout's captured trace data in one palm, analysing it slowly. Light particles trace a complex pattern.

**SHIRO** *(eyes narrowing)*: "...As I suspected. The way these scouts move — **they blank out the map first, then the main body advances**. Ghost always sends reconnaissance ahead. Make things invisible — then move in."

**PICO** *(leaning in)*: "So Ghost was actually here?"
**SHIRO**: "Only the scouts. — But it's getting closer. Ghost is building a map right now. Somewhere. A very *large* map."

Silence.

**PICO**: "...Shiro. Why does Ghost blank out the map? Why make things *invisible* first, instead of just coming straight in?"
**SHIRO** *(after a pause)*: "— **If you can't see it, you can't defend it**. If the defenders can't hold their own map, they won't notice the attack no matter which direction it comes from. Ghost... knows that very well."

Pico is quiet, turning those words over.

**PICO**: "...So what we did today — building our own map first — that's the very first answer to Ghost?"
**SHIRO** *(quietly)*: "— Exactly."

Pico looks out at the port in the dawn light. Containers moving. A ship coming in. All of it visible. All of it *seen*.

**PICO** *(small voice, but clear)*: "...Turns out my old tricks work just fine for protecting things."

Shiro doesn't answer — just produces two tickets with a quiet flick of the wrist.

**SHIRO**: "Let's go, Pico. Next city."
**PICO**: "What about the chicken rice?!"
**SHIRO**: "...At the airport."
**PICO**: "YES!!" *(breaks into a jog to keep up)*

**[FINAL SHOT]** The surface of the harbour water. A white thermal afterimage of Ghost shimmers and sways — and its outline is shaped like **an enormous map**. It fades. Quietly. Gone.

## 🔎 Field Guide Corner (30 seconds — channel format)

Back in chibi mode, the two stand in front of a whiteboard. Pico is holding a marker.

**PICO**: "So what even IS this 'en-map' thing today?"
**SHIRO**: "**Nmap — Network Mapper**. It's a tool that scans your own network to find out which devices are connected and which ports are open, then turns all of that into a map."
**PICO**: "It's something attackers can use too, though, right...?"
**SHIRO**: "Right. That's exactly why it's important for defenders to **use it on themselves first**. Build your own map before the attacker can — that's what we call a **'self-diagnostic'**. It falls under the category of **Reconnaissance and Asset Management**."
**PICO** *(writing on the whiteboard: "Build your own map first!")*: "It's all about HOW you use it!"
**SHIRO**: "By the way, in real-world operations, Nmap results are combined with **vulnerability scanners like Tenable or Qualys** to check for any gaps in the defences."

## Production Notes (Self-Review)

**Action Grammar**: Root Silence follows the charge → release → stillness structure and matches the signature still-frame format. Self-Mapping is differentiated from the blunder through the "quiet and careful" contrast. **Mistake Pattern**: Pico tries to repurpose an offensive skill for defence but runs it the same way as an attack — causing the defensive system to misidentify it as a threat. Mirrors Episode 1's structure symmetrically ("right tool, wrong approach" vs. Episode 1's "right goal, wrong scale"). **Real-World Cyber**: Nmap (reconnaissance / asset management) + Tenable/Qualys (vulnerability scanners); C2-severing structure consistent with Episode 1. **7+ Safety**: Scouts are cute cyan particles with round "fnyah?" eyes; neutralised = captured (not destroyed); zero blood, zero horror, zero occult imagery. **Vertical Arc**: Ghost's "recon-first, then advance" structure is made explicit; the "Ghost is building a large map somewhere" thread is planted. **Character Growth**: "Turns out my old tricks work just fine for protecting things" — the embryo of Pico's defining line, landing naturally at Episode 8's position on the series-long growth ladder.
